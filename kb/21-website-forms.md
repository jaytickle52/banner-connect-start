# Put your inquiry form on your website

Your website form is how most families first reach you. Banner Connect gives you a short piece of code for each form in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, and every family who fills it out lands in your account with their follow-up already started.

## Which form should go on our website?

Most schools put **Request Info Packet** on their admissions page and **General Inquiry Form** on their contact page. Request Info Packet asks what the family wants to know (admissions, tuition and aid, academics, athletics or spiritual life). General Inquiry Form asks whether they're a prospective family, a current family or someone else, so the right person sees it.

Two more forms are worth a spot on the site:

- **Request a Visit** on a "Visit us" page, for families who want you to call them to set up a time. To let families pick a time themselves, see [Let families book a campus visit](/help/articles/campus-visits/).
- **Request Tuition Info** and **Request Financial Aid Info** on your tuition page.

Keep the forms short. Each one asks only for the parent's name, email, phone and how many children they're thinking about enrolling, plus one or two questions. Every extra field you add means fewer families finish it. To put a form on a page, see [Put your inquiry form on your website](#put-your-inquiry-form-on-your-website).

## Put your inquiry form on your website

You copy the form's embed code from **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, and paste it on the page where families should see it.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, open the **Prebuilt Forms** folder and open the form (for example **Request Info Packet**).
2. Click **Integrate** in the top right, choose the embed option and click **Copy**.
3. Paste the code on your website page:
   - WordPress: add a Custom HTML block (in Elementor, an HTML widget; in Divi, a Code module).
   - Squarespace: add a Code block.
   - Wix: add Embed, then Embed HTML.
   - Webflow and other builders: add an Embed or custom code element.
4. Publish the page.
5. Open the live page in a new window. Some website editors don't show the form in preview, only on the published page.

If someone else manages your website, send them the code and tell them which page it goes on. If you'd rather not do it yourself, share access to your website with the Banner 61 team and we'll add it.

How to check it worked: the form shows on your live page, and a test submission shows up in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, on the **Submissions** tab. See [Test your website form](#test-your-website-form).

## Link to a form when you can't edit your website

If you can't paste code on your website, link a button to a page that holds only the form.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, and open the form.
2. Click **Integrate** and copy the form's link instead of the embed code.
3. On your website, social post or email, point a button or link (for example "Request info") at that link.

Your account also has ready-made form pages for Request Info Packet, General Inquiry Form, Request Tuition Info and Request Financial Aid Info under **Marketing**, then **Funnels**. Open the page, copy its address and use it the same way.

How to check it worked: click your new button on the live site and the form opens.

## Test your website form

Fill out the form on your live website once with your own name and email, then check that it arrived in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**.

1. Open your live website page and fill out the form with your own name and a real email you can check.
2. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, and click the **Submissions** tab.
3. Choose the form at the top left. Your test should be at the top of the list.
4. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and search your name. Your answers are on your contact record.
5. When you're done, delete your test submission and your test contact so they don't count in your reports or get your school's follow-up emails.

Before you delete anything else, check with the Banner 61 team.

How to check it worked: your test shows on the Submissions tab with today's date. To learn what else happens when a form comes in, see [What happens when a family fills out a form](/help/articles/form-submissions/).

## Why isn't our form showing on our website?

Most of the time the page wasn't published, or the website editor removed the code. Check these in order:

- **You're looking at a preview.** Many website editors only show embedded forms on the published page. Publish and check the live page.
- **The code was pasted in the wrong place.** It has to go in a code, HTML or embed block, not a regular text block, or the page shows the code as words.
- **Part of the code got cut off.** Copy it again from **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, then **Integrate**, and paste the whole thing.
- **The page has a security setting that blocks outside forms.** Ask whoever manages your website, or the Banner 61 team.

If you changed the form's fields, the change shows on your website right away. You don't need to paste the code again. To fix a form that loads but doesn't send, see [Test your website form](#test-your-website-form).
