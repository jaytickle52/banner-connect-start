# Put parent videos on your website

Parent videos go on your website through a video widget: a short piece of code that shows the videos parents sent through your **Parent Video Stories** link. The Banner 61 team builds the widget for you, and you paste its code on a page the same way you add a form.

## Put parent videos on your website

Ask the Banner 61 team for a video widget, then paste the code they send on your website page.

1. Make sure at least two or three parents have sent videos. You can see them in **Marketing**, then **Video Testimonials**, then the **Responses** tab.
2. Ask the Banner 61 team for a video widget for **Parent Video Stories**. Tell them which page it's for and which videos to include.
3. Paste the code they send on your website page:
   - WordPress: add a Custom HTML block (in Elementor, an HTML widget; in Divi, a Code module).
   - Squarespace: add a Code block.
   - Wix: add Embed, then Embed HTML.
   - Webflow and other builders: add an Embed or custom code element.
4. Publish the page.

If someone else manages your website, send them the code and tell them which page it goes on. Or share access to your website with the Banner 61 team and they'll add it.

How to check it worked: open the live page in a new window, not the editor preview, and play a video. Some website editors only show the widget on the published page.

## Where should parent videos go on our website?

Put parent videos where a family is deciding whether to take the next step. The admissions page, right above your inquiry form, is the best spot, because a parent's story answers "is this the right school for us?" just before the family reaches out.

Other good places are the homepage, the tuition page (a parent on why it's worth it) and your "Visit us" page. One to three videos on a page is plenty. To add the widget to a page, see [Put parent videos on your website](#put-parent-videos-on-your-website).

## Choose which videos show on your website

Only show videos the parent agreed to share. Before a video goes on your website, check its consent in **Marketing**, then **Video Testimonials**, then **Responses**, using the **Marketing consent** filter.

When you ask the Banner 61 team for a widget, tell them which videos to include. New videos don't need a new code: once the widget is on your page, the Banner 61 team can add or remove videos and your page updates on its own.

For more on permission, see [Do we need permission to share a parent's video?](/help/articles/parent-video-stories/#do-we-need-permission-to-share-a-parents-video)

## Take a parent's video off our website

If a parent asks you to remove their video, tell the Banner 61 team which video it is and they'll take it out of your widget. Also remove it anywhere else you've used it, such as social posts or emails.

You don't need to change the code on your website. The widget updates on its own.

How to check it worked: open the live page in a private browser window and confirm the video is gone.

## We don't have any parent videos yet. Where do we start?

Start by asking two or three parents you know love the school. Send each one the **Parent Video Stories** link with a short, personal note. See [Send a parent the video link](/help/articles/parent-video-stories/#send-a-parent-the-video-link) and [What should we say when we ask a parent?](/help/articles/parent-video-stories/#what-should-we-say-when-we-ask-a-parent)
