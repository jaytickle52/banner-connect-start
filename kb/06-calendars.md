# Calendars

The Calendars tab has four pages:
- **Calendar View**: everyone coming in, by day or week.
- **Appointment List**: every booking in a list you can search and filter.
- **Meeting Calendars**: your booking pages and their settings.
- **Connections**: link your Google, Outlook or iCloud calendar.

## Your prebuilt calendars

- **Schedule a Visit**: families book a campus visit. Booking here sends a confirmation and reminders automatically, and moves the family to Visit Scheduled in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline. After the visit, your staff get an email with links to mark what happened (see [After a visit: the staff follow-up email](/help/articles/automations/#after-a-visit-the-staff-follow-up-email)).
- **Event Registration**: for open houses, preview days and information nights. Families pick from the dates you add.
- **Schedule an Assessment**: turned off until your school needs it.

## Connect your calendar

Connecting your own calendar keeps online bookings from landing on top of things you already have.

1. Go to **Calendars**, then **Connections**.
2. Click to connect Google or Outlook and sign in with the account you use for appointments.
3. Choose which of your calendars Banner Connect should check for conflicts, so personal and school events block those times.
4. Choose the calendar new visits should be added to.

Then go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, open **Schedule a Visit**, and make sure the days and hours match when you actually give visits.

Tip: link your work calendar both ways so new visits appear there. Add a personal calendar as a conflict calendar only, so it blocks time without showing your private events. Each staff member can review their own calendar connections in **Settings**, then **My Profile**.

## Book a visit for a family (phone or walk-in)

1. Go to **Calendars**, then **Calendar View**, and make sure you're looking at Schedule a Visit.
2. Click a time slot, or click **+ New**.
3. Check that Calendar is set to **Schedule a Visit**.
4. Search for the family by name or email. If they're new, click **Add New Contact** and enter name, email and phone.
5. Confirm the date and time, add any notes (grade of interest, who referred them) and save.

The family gets the same confirmation and reminders as if they'd booked online, and moves to Visit Scheduled. This only happens on the Schedule a Visit calendar, so always check which calendar is selected.

You can also book from a contact record: open the Appointments icon in the right panel.

## Change available times

Go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, and click the pencil next to the calendar. You can change the meeting length, the days and hours families can book, and add date-specific hours for holidays or event days. Under the advanced settings you'll also find the description families see, the location (your school address, a phone call or an online meeting), how far ahead families can book, minimum notice, buffers between appointments, daily limits, the booking form, and reminder and cancellation settings.

## Set up an event (open house)

Before you share the Event Registration calendar:

1. **Edit the description** so it matches your event.
2. **Add each event date**: go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, click the pencil next to Event Registration, go to **Availability**, click **Add Date Specific Hours**, pick the date, set the start and end time, and save. Repeat for each date.
3. **Confirm who gets the staff email**: go to **Automations**, open the **Event Registration** workflow, find the **Internal Staff Email** step, check the email address and save.
4. **Review the event emails** in **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**: Event Registration Confirmation Email #1, 24 Hours Before #2, 1 Hour Before #3, After Visit Internal Notification #4, Reschedule Visit Email #5, Thank You Email with Next Steps #6 and Visit Feedback Survey Email #7. Pay the most attention to #6, which tells families what to do next after the event.

When a family registers, Banner Connect adds them to New Inquiry (if they aren't in the pipeline yet), tags them Event Registration, sends a confirmation, a reminder 24 hours before and another 1 hour before. After the event your staff get an email with three links: they showed up (sends next steps and the feedback survey), they didn't show (sends a "sorry we missed you" email with a link to book a visit), or they showed but aren't a fit (no email). Click one per family.

Want a full event page with a cover image, FAQs, a guest list and check-in? See [Open houses, Vision Nights and Promoter Receptions](/help/articles/events/). An Events RSVP doesn't start the Event Registration workflow, so use this calendar when you want families added to your pipeline automatically.

## Share or embed a calendar

From **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, open the calendar's share options to:
- copy the booking link to send by email or text,
- copy the embed code to put the calendar on your website,
- create a one-time link for a single family.

## Can't see something?

- **Appointments missing from the calendar**: check the filter panel on the right side of Calendar View and select all the staff you want to see.
- **A calendar is missing**: go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**. Every calendar is listed there.
