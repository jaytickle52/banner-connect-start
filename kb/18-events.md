# Open houses, Vision Nights and Promoter Receptions

Events gives each gathering its own page with a cover image, details, FAQs and an RSVP button, plus a list of who's coming and QR check-in at the door. It lives in **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**. Banner 61 turns Events on for your school, so if you don't see it, ask the Banner 61 team.

## Which kind of event should I create?

Most schools run three kinds of events, and all three are free RSVP events:

- **Open House**: for prospective families. They tour campus, visit classrooms, meet teachers and the admissions team, and ask about tuition and aid.
- **Vision Night**: an evening where your head of school shares where the school is headed. Invite current families, supporters and interested prospective families.
- **Promoter Reception**: a thank-you gathering for your most enthusiastic current families, the ones most likely to recommend you. Use it to thank them and make it easy for them to invite friends to your next open house.

Choose **RSVP** for all three. Choose **Ticketed** only when families pay to attend, which needs a payment account connected; check with the Banner 61 team first.

To build one, see [Create an open house page](#create-an-open-house-page).

## Create an open house page

Each event starts as a draft that only your staff can see, so you can build it without anyone finding it early. The same steps work for a Vision Night or a Promoter Reception.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, and click **Create Event**.
2. Choose **RSVP**, then click **Continue**.
3. Type the event name families will see, such as "Fall Open House," "Spring Vision Night" or "Promoter Reception."
4. Add a cover image. Upload a photo or graphic up to 5 MB, ideally 2160 by 1080 pixels. When asked, click the most important part of the image (usually the headline or faces) so it stays visible when the image is cropped.
5. Write a short summary of one sentence and a fuller description. Lead with what families will do, for example: "Tour campus, meet our teachers, and learn about enrollment."
6. Click **Continue**. Set the date, start time and end time. Check that the time zone is your school's.
7. Click **Continue**. Choose **In-person** and search for your school's address, or choose **Online**.
8. Click **Continue**. Under organizer information, enter your school's name and the admissions email families should write to.
9. Click **Create event**.

How to check it worked: the event opens on its **Overview** tab with a gray **Draft** label beside the name, and it appears in **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**.

## Add FAQs to an event page

FAQs answer the questions families ask before they come, so fewer of them email you.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, and open the event.
2. Click the **Pages & design** tab, then the **Details** tab inside it.
3. Under **Page sections**, find **Frequently asked questions** and click the plus beside it. Make sure its switch is on.
4. Click **Add question**. Type the question, then the answer. Repeat for each question.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

Good questions for an open house: Who should attend? What will we do? Do I need to RSVP? Is there a cost? Will you talk about tuition and financial aid? What if I can't make it? For a Vision Night, add what will be shared and whether childcare is provided. For a Promoter Reception, add who is invited and how to bring a friend to the next open house.

How to check it worked: the questions appear in a "Frequently asked questions" section in the page preview on the right.

## Choose the questions on the RSVP form

Every RSVP form asks for first name, last name and email. You can add more questions, and the school's own family fields save the answer straight to the family's record in **CRM**, then **Contacts**.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, and open the event.
2. Click the **Pages & design** tab, then the **Checkout** tab inside it.
3. Click **Add fields** and pick a field. Fields listed under **Contact custom fields** are the same family fields your inquiry forms use, so answers land on the family's record.
4. Tick **Required** beside a field only if you truly need it. Shorter forms get more RSVPs.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

For an open house, add **Phone**, **How many children are you interested in enrolling?**, **Child 1 Name** and **Child 1 Grade of Entry**. A Vision Night or Promoter Reception usually needs only phone and the number attending. Don't create new fields from this screen; ask the Banner 61 team, because other forms and automations depend on your field names.

How to check it worked: the new questions appear on the form preview on the right.

## Publish an event and share the link

Publishing makes the page public and opens RSVPs. Publish only when everything is ready, because a published event can't be switched back to a draft.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, and open the event.
2. Click **Preview** to see the page the way families will.
3. Click **Publish event**.
4. To share it, go back to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, switch to the list view, click the three dots beside the event and choose **Copy event link**. Put the link in your newsletter, on your website and in social posts. The **Open House Invitation** email template in **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**, then the **Campaigns** folder is a good place to start the invitation.

How to check it worked: the label beside the event name changes to green **Published**, and the copied link opens the page with an RSVP button.

## See who RSVPed and check families in

Every RSVP lands in the event's attendee list.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, and open the event.
2. Click the **Registrations & check-in** tab. **Registrations** lists everyone who RSVPed, with the date. Use **Export CSV** to download the list, or **Add Attendee** to add a family who RSVPed by phone.
3. At the event, use the **Check-in** tab to mark families as they arrive. QR code check-in is on for in-person events.

How to check it worked: a family who RSVPs shows up in **Registrations** with a status, and shows as checked in once you've checked them in.

## Close RSVPs without taking the page down

Set the registration deadline to stop new RSVPs. The page stays up and shows "Registration closed." This is also how to pause an event that was published too early, since a published event can't go back to draft.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, and open the event.
2. Click the **Settings** tab, then **Registration**.
3. Set **Deadline date** and **Deadline time** to when RSVPs should close. To close them right away, pick a time that has already passed.
4. Click **Save changes**.

To cap the number of guests instead, choose **Set a limit** under **Venue Capacity**.

How to check it worked: open the event link. The RSVP button is gone and the page says "Registration closed."

## What emails do families get?

Events sends its own emails. In **Calendars and Events**, then **Events**, open the event, click **Settings**, then **Notifications**. There you can turn on or off:

- a confirmation as soon as a family RSVPs,
- a reminder 3 days before the event,
- a message if the date, time or place changes,
- a thank-you with a feedback survey 1 hour after the event ends,
- a new RSVP alert and a daily summary for your staff.

Changing the wording of these emails is safe. Turning them off means families get nothing from Banner Connect about the event.

## Should I use Events or the Event Registration calendar?

Use Events when you want a polished page for one gathering, with an image, FAQs, a guest list and check-in. Use the **Event Registration** calendar in **Calendars and Events**, then **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, when you want families added to your enrollment pipeline automatically.

The difference matters for follow-up. A family who books through the Event Registration calendar starts the Event Registration workflow: they're added to New Inquiry in Inquiry to Enrollment, tagged, reminded and followed up after the event. An Events RSVP doesn't start that workflow, so a new family won't appear in your pipeline on their own. Check the RSVP list after each event and add new families, or ask the Banner 61 team about connecting Events to your follow-up. See [Set up an event (open house)](/help/articles/calendars/#set-up-an-event-open-house) for the calendar way.
