# Run an open house with sign-ups and reminders

Families sign up for an open house on the **Event Registration** calendar, and Banner Connect handles the confirmation, the reminders and the follow-up. You add the date, share the sign-up link, invite families, and after the event you mark who came. Event Registration lives in **Calendars and Events**, then **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**.

## Add your open house date and time

Each open house is a date you add to the Event Registration calendar.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**.
2. Click the pencil next to **Event Registration**.
3. Open **Availability** and click **Add Date Specific Hours**.
4. Pick the date and set the start and end time, for example 6:00 to 7:30 pm.
5. Save. Repeat for each open house date this year.

How to check it worked: open the Event Registration sign-up link in a private browser window. Your date and time should be the only choices, shown in your school's time zone.

## Write the event description families see

The description is what families read before they sign up.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**.
2. Click the pencil next to **Event Registration**.
3. Edit the description. Say what families will do (tour classrooms, meet teachers, hear how admissions works), who it's for and how long it lasts.
4. Save.

If you list a date in the description, update it after every event. A description without a date never goes out of date.

How to check it worked: open the sign-up link and read it as a parent would.

## Invite families to sign up

Send an email campaign with the sign-up link to the families you want to invite.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, open the share options next to **Event Registration** and copy the link.
2. Go to **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**, and open **Open House Invitation** in the **Campaigns** folder.
3. Fill in the date, time and details, and paste the sign-up link into the button.
4. Send it to a smart list, such as families in New Inquiry. See [Email campaigns](/help/articles/marketing/#email-campaigns-one-time-emails-to-a-group).

You can also add the sign-up link to your website and social posts.

How to check it worked: send yourself a test email and click the button.

## What happens when a family signs up?

When a family signs up on Event Registration, Banner Connect adds them to New Inquiry in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline (if they aren't there yet), tags them Event Registration and sends a confirmation email. A reminder goes out 24 hours before the event and another 1 hour before. The family sees a short "You're registered" message on screen right away. Signing up also adds 5 points to their [engagement score](/help/articles/engagement-score/).

These steps run from the Event Registration workflow. Check with the Banner 61 team before changing it. See [What's already built](/help/articles/automations/#whats-already-built).

## See who signed up

Every sign-up appears as a booking on the Event Registration calendar.

1. Go to **Calendars and Events**, then **Calendars**, then **Appointment List**.
2. Filter to the **Event Registration** calendar and the event date.

Print or export the list for a name-tag table or a sign-in sheet.

How to check it worked: the number of bookings should match the sign-up count you expect.

## Mark who came after the open house

After the event, your staff get an email with three links for each family who signed up: they showed up, they didn't show, or they showed but aren't a fit. Click one link per family.

- **Showed up**: the family gets a thank-you email with next steps and, an hour later, the feedback survey. They gain 15 points on their engagement score.
- **Didn't show**: the family gets a "sorry we missed you" email with a link to book a campus visit.
- **Showed, not a fit**: no email goes out.

Check who gets the staff email: go to **Automations**, then **Workflows**, open **Event Registration** and look at the **Internal Staff Email** step. Ask the Banner 61 team before changing anything else in that workflow.

How to check it worked: open a family who came and confirm their visit shows as Showed.

## Should I use this or an Events page?

Use the Event Registration calendar when you want families who sign up added to your enrollment pipeline, reminded and followed up automatically. An Events page gives you a polished page with an image, FAQs and check-in, but its RSVPs don't start that follow-up. See [Should I use Events or the Event Registration calendar?](/help/articles/events/#should-i-use-events-or-the-event-registration-calendar)

## Plan the next open house

Add the next date as soon as one event ends, so families who find you between events can still sign up. Remove past dates from **Availability** on the Event Registration calendar and update the description if it mentions a date.
