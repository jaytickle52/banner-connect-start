# What happens when a family fills out a form

When a family submits one of your forms, Banner Connect saves their answers, files them on the family's record and starts that form's follow-up, without anyone on your staff lifting a finger. Every submission is listed in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, on the **Submissions** tab.

## What happens when a family fills out a form?

Four things happen within a minute of a family clicking submit:

1. **Their record is created or updated.** A new family gets a new contact. A family already in Banner Connect is matched by email or phone, and their record is updated instead of copied.
2. **Their answers are saved.** Each answer lands in a field on the family's record (for example child name and grade of entry), and the whole submission shows on the **Submissions** tab and on the family's record.
3. **Their engagement score goes up.** Request a Visit adds 10 points. General Inquiry Form, Request Info Packet, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info and the Event Registration Form add 5. See [What adds points to a family's score](/help/articles/engagement-score/#what-adds-points-to-a-familys-score).
4. **The form's follow-up starts**, if your school has it turned on: a tag, an enrollment card in New Inquiry or a series of emails, depending on the form. See [Which follow-up does each form start?](#which-follow-up-does-each-form-start)

To see the submission for yourself, see [See who filled out a form this week](#see-who-filled-out-a-form-this-week).

## Which follow-up does each form start?

Each prebuilt form has its own workflow in **Automations**, then **Workflows**. Which ones are turned on depends on your school's setup, so ask the Banner 61 team if you're not sure.

| Form | What usually happens next |
|---|---|
| General Inquiry Form | Adds the General Inquiry tag. If the parent chose "Prospective Family," the family gets a card in New Inquiry on Inquiry to Enrollment |
| Request Info Packet | The family gets a card in New Inquiry and a short series of follow-up emails |
| Request Tuition Info | Sends your tuition information email |
| Request Financial Aid Info | Sends your financial aid email |
| Request a Visit | Saves the request so your staff can call the family. A visit is only booked, and the family only moves to Visit Scheduled, when it's put on the Schedule a Visit calendar |
| Event Registration Form | Used on the Event Registration calendar. The registration, not the form, sends the confirmation and reminders |
| Sibling Intent Form | Saves the sibling's details for your admissions team |

A family who already has an open card on Inquiry to Enrollment doesn't get a second one. Their existing card stays where it is.

Turning a workflow on or off, or changing what a form starts, affects every family who fills it out. Check with the Banner 61 team before you change one. For how workflows work, see [Automations](/help/articles/automations/).

## Does a form make a duplicate family?

No. When a family already in Banner Connect fills out a form with the same email or phone, Banner Connect updates their existing record instead of making a new one.

What does change is any field they answered differently. If a parent types a new phone number or a different grade, the new answer replaces the old one on their record. If a parent uses a different email and phone than last time, Banner Connect can't tell it's the same family, and you'll see two records. To combine them, see [Duplicates](/help/articles/contacts/#duplicates).

## See who filled out a form this week

Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, then the **Submissions** tab, and choose the form you want.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**.
2. Click the **Submissions** tab.
3. Choose the form at the top left (for example **Request a Visit**).
4. Set the dates at the top right to this week. The list shows the newest first.
5. Click a family's name to open their record and call or email them.

To keep a copy, click **Export** to download the list.

How to check it worked: the families who filled out that form in those dates are listed, each with the date and time they submitted.

## See everything a family has sent you

Open the family's record in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and their form submissions sit in the timeline with every email, text and booking.

1. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and search the parent's name or email.
2. Open their record. The center of the page shows every form submission, email, text and booking, newest at the bottom.
3. On the right, open **Activity** to see the forms they submitted and the pages they visited.

How to check it worked: the form the family filled out shows with the date and their answers.

## Why didn't a family get the follow-up email?

Usually the form's workflow isn't turned on, the family is set to Do Not Disturb, or they already got that email once. A submission always saves, even when no email goes out, so you'll still see it on the **Submissions** tab in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**.

Other common reasons:

- The workflow for that form is in Draft in **Automations**, then **Workflows**.
- The family unsubscribed from your emails earlier.
- The email went to their spam folder.
- They filled out a form that doesn't send an email, such as Request a Visit or the Sibling Intent Form.

For the full list and how to check, see ["Why didn't this send?"](/help/articles/automations/#why-didnt-this-send). Before you turn a workflow on, check with the Banner 61 team.
