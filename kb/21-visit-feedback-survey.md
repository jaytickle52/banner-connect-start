# Send the visit feedback survey automatically after every tour

Every family who tours your school can get a short feedback survey by email without anyone on your staff writing it. It goes out as soon as you mark the visit as attended and a good fit, and the answers land in **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, then **Submissions**. The survey is called **Visit Feedback**.

## What sends the visit feedback survey?

The Visit Feedback survey sends when a staff member marks a family as "showed and a good fit" after a visit. Nothing else is needed.

After each visit on the Schedule a Visit calendar, the staff member who gave it gets an email with three links. Clicking **They showed and are a good fit** moves the family to Visit Attended in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline and sends the **Visit Feedback Survey Email #7**, which links to the survey. Open houses work the same way: when a family is marked as showing up on the Event Registration calendar, the feedback survey email follows an hour after the thank-you email.

Families marked as not a fit or as no-shows don't get the survey. These steps run from the Request a Visit and Event Registration workflows in **Automations**, then **Workflows**. Check with the Banner 61 team before changing either one. See [Make sure every family who tours gets the survey](#make-sure-every-family-who-tours-gets-the-survey).

## Make sure every family who tours gets the survey

The survey only goes out when someone clicks the link in the staff email, so the habit to build is one click per visit, the same day.

1. After each visit, open the email Banner Connect sends the staff member who gave the visit.
2. Click the link that fits: **They showed and are a good fit**, **They showed but aren't a good fit**, or **They didn't show**.
3. The link opens Banner Connect so you can see the result.

If the visit email went to the wrong person, go to **Automations**, then **Workflows**, open **Request a Visit** and look at the staff email step to see who it goes to. Ask the Banner 61 team to change it.

How to check it worked: open the family in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**. Their card should be in Visit Attended, and their visit should show as Showed.

## Read what families said about their visit

All survey answers are in one place: **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, then the **Submissions** tab.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**.
2. Click the **Submissions** tab.
3. Choose **Visit Feedback** in the survey filter and set the date range you want.
4. Click a response to see the full answers: the star rating, how the family feels about your school as a fit, what stood out, what they wished they'd seen, and what they'd like next.

Read them weekly during visit season. A run of three-star ratings or the same thing "wished we'd seen" is something to change in how you give visits.

How to check it worked: the family's name shows on each response. If a response has no name, the answers weren't tied to the family. Tell the Banner 61 team so they can check how the survey link is sent.

## Follow up when a family asks for a conversation

The last question on the survey asks what would help most as a next step. When a family checks **A follow-up conversation**, call or email them within a day, while the visit is fresh.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, then the **Submissions** tab, and filter to **Visit Feedback**.
2. Open each new response and look at the next-step question.
3. For a family who asked for a conversation, open their contact and call, text or email from **CRM**, then **Conversations**.
4. If you can't reach them today, add a task on their contact so it doesn't get lost. See [Tasks](/help/articles/tasks/).

Filling out the survey also adds 5 points to the family's [engagement score](/help/articles/engagement-score/), so families who answer rise toward the top of your call list.

How to check it worked: the family's conversation shows your call note, text or email.

## Change the survey questions

You can edit the Visit Feedback survey yourself in **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**. It sits in the **Prebuilt Surveys** folder.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, and open the **Prebuilt Surveys** folder.
2. Click **Visit Feedback** to open the survey builder.
3. Click any question to change its wording or answer choices. Keep the next-step question; it's how families ask for a follow-up.
4. Click **Save**. Changes are live for families right away.

Keep it short. The survey promises families about two minutes, and every question you add makes fewer families finish it.

How to check it worked: click **Preview** at the top of the survey builder and walk through it as a parent would. Close the preview without submitting, so you don't add a test response.
