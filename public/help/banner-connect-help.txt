# About Banner Connect

Banner Connect is the enrollment CRM Banner 61 sets up for Christian schools. It keeps every family, message, visit and follow-up in one place, from the first inquiry to an enrolled student. Banner 61 builds each school's account with forms, a pipeline, calendars, email templates and automations already in place, so most schools start by reviewing what's there rather than building from scratch.

Sign in at crm.banner61.com. On a phone, use the LeadConnector app (search for it in the App Store or Google Play) with the same email and password.

## The menu

Every Banner Connect account has the same menu, in this order:

| Tab | What's inside |
|---|---|
| Getting Started | Plain-language guide to every tab, with one-minute demos and setup guides |
| Dashboard | Enrollment at a glance: funnel, tuition in the pipeline, tasks |
| Ask AI | Ask questions about your account or how to do something |
| CRM | Contacts (with Smart Lists, Bulk Actions, Tags), Conversations (Inbox, Snippets, Trigger Links), Opportunities (your pipeline), Tasks, Students |
| Calendars | Calendar View, Meeting Calendars, Connections, Appointment List |
| Forms & Docs | Forms, Surveys, Documents & Contracts |
| Marketing | Social Planner, Emails, Trigger Links, Video Testimonials, Funnels, QR Codes |
| Automations | Workflows (the follow-up that runs on its own) |
| Reporting | Custom Reports, Reports, SEO, Site Analytics, External Tracking |
| Payments | Invoices, Payment Links, Transactions, Payment Integrations and more |
| Advanced | Custom Fields, Custom Values, Object Settings, Media Storage, Domains, Brand Boards |
| Settings | Business Profile, My Profile, Users, Communications (phone and email), Integrations, Audit Logs |

Trigger Links appear in two places (CRM, then Conversations, and Marketing). They're the same list.

## Words Banner Connect uses

- **Contact**: one person, usually a parent. Every form, email and booking connects to a contact.
- **Opportunity**: one family's place in your enrollment process. It's the card you see on the pipeline board.
- **Pipeline**: the columns a family moves through, such as New Inquiry, Visit Scheduled and Enrolled.
- **Stage**: one column in a pipeline.
- **Status**: Open, Won (enrolled), Lost or Abandoned. Status is separate from stage.
- **Tag**: a label on a contact, such as Event Registration or Cold Inquiry Revival. Tags can start automations.
- **Smart List**: a saved filter of contacts that updates on its own.
- **Workflow**: an automation. It starts when something happens (a form is submitted, a visit is booked) and then sends emails, adds tags, creates tasks and so on.
- **Template**: a saved email that workflows and campaigns reuse.
- **Snippet**: a saved reply you can drop into a message.
- **Custom field**: an extra detail you track, like Grade(s) of Entry.
- **Custom value**: a school detail used in every email, like your school's name, address or phone number.

## Names in your account

Banner 61 sets up every account with the same names. Use them exactly, because automations depend on them.

- **Forms** (Forms & Docs, then Forms): General Inquiry Form, Request Info Packet, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info, Request a Visit, Event Registration Form, Sibling Intent Form. See [Your prebuilt forms](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/forms-and-docs/#your-prebuilt-forms).
- **Calendars** (Calendars, then Meeting Calendars): Schedule a Visit (campus visits), Event Registration (open houses and events), Schedule an Assessment (off until needed). See [Your prebuilt calendars](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/calendars/#your-prebuilt-calendars).
- **Pipelines** (CRM, then Opportunities): Inquiry to Enrollment (New Inquiry, Visit Scheduled, Visit Attended, Application Sent, Application Submitted, Accepted, Enrolled), Re-Enrollment Pipeline, Sibling Enrollment Fast Track. See [Your pipelines](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/pipeline/#your-pipelines).
- **Smart lists** (CRM, then Contacts): Current Families (everyone in the Enrolled stage) and Stalled Leads (open families in Inquiry to Enrollment with no email, text or call in 14 days). See [Smart Lists](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/smart-lists/).
- **Workflows** (Automations, then Workflows): Request Info Packet / Lead Nurture, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info, General Inquiry, Request a Visit, Event Registration, Cold Inquiry Revival, Stalled Leads / Remove Stalled Tag, Engagement score: application submitted. See [What's already built](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/automations/#whats-already-built).
- **Email templates** (Marketing, then Emails, then Templates): one folder per workflow above, plus a **Campaigns** folder with Open House Invitation, Monthly Family Newsletter, Re-enrollment Is Open and Quick Announcement for one-time sends. See [Email campaigns](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/marketing/#email-campaigns-one-time-emails-to-a-group).
- **Lost reasons** (on an opportunity, when Status is Lost): Chose another school, Cost, Moved, Not a fit, No response. See [Mark a family who isn't enrolling](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/enrollment-stages/#mark-a-family-who-isnt-enrolling).
- **Tags that start automations**: Cold Inquiry Revival (a series of re-engagement emails). Check before adding any tag.

## What Banner 61 handles

Banner 61 sets up the account, the prebuilt forms, workflows, templates and pipeline. Banner 61 can also add your inquiry form to your website, create new custom fields, attach your info packets to emails, add users, and change account-level settings. When a school isn't sure whether something is safe to change, the answer is to ask the Banner 61 team first.

Some things only the school can do, because they sign in to the school's own accounts: connecting Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile, connecting a staff member's email or calendar, and connecting a payment account.

# Getting started

## Logging in for the first time

About 48 to 72 hours after a school signs up, each user gets an email with the subject "Activate Your Account | Banner 61". It contains their Email ID and a **Click Here to Login** button.

1. Click **Click Here to Login**.
2. Set a password. It needs upper and lower case letters, at least one number and one symbol, and at least 8 characters.
3. Click **Change Password**, then sign in at crm.banner61.com with your email and the new password.
4. The first time you sign in on a new device, Banner Connect emails you a security code. Enter it to finish signing in.

Can't find the activation email or the security code? Check Promotions and Spam. In Gmail, click More at the bottom of the left menu to see them. Move the email to your Primary inbox (or click Not spam) so future Banner Connect emails arrive normally.

## The first four things to do

The Getting Started tab at the top of the menu walks through these with a short guide for each.

1. **Put your inquiry form on your website.** This is how families reach you. See [Put your inquiry form on your website](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/forms-and-docs/#put-your-inquiry-form-on-your-website) for the steps. Most schools finish in ten minutes, and Banner 61 will do it for you if you share access to your website.
2. **Connect Facebook, Instagram and Google.** Messages from your pages then land in one inbox with your email and texts. Someone at the school does this, because it signs in to the school's own accounts. See [Connect Facebook, Instagram and Google](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/conversations/#connect-facebook-instagram-and-google).
3. **Connect your calendar.** Visits booked online then fit around what's already on your calendar. See [Connect your calendar](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/calendars/#connect-your-calendar).
4. **Install the mobile app.** Search for LeadConnector in the App Store or Google Play, sign in with your Banner Connect email and password, choose your school if asked, and allow notifications.

## Connect your email (two-way sync)

Connecting your inbox means emails you send from Banner Connect show up in your normal sent mail, and replies from families show up inside Banner Connect.

1. Click **Settings**, then **My Profile**.
2. Scroll to **Email (2-way sync)**.
3. Choose Gmail or Outlook and click **Connect**.
4. Pick your email account and click **Allow**.

Syncing then runs on its own in the background. Each staff member connects their own inbox.

## Before your forms go live: review and approve

Banner 61 builds your forms, emails and visit survey before launch. Review the ones you plan to use, then tell the Banner 61 team they're approved. You only need to review what you'll actually use.

**Forms.** Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**. The prebuilt forms are General Inquiry Form (contact form), Request a Visit, Request Financial Aid Info, Request Info Packet and Request Tuition Info. Open each one you'll use and check the fields and labels.

**Email templates.** Go to **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then the **Templates** tab. Templates are grouped in folders that match the workflows that send them:
- **Request Info Packet / Lead Nurture**: a short series that goes to families who submit the Request Info Packet form.
- **Request Tuition Info**: one email, sent when the Request Tuition Info form is submitted.
- **Request Financial Aid Info**: one email, sent when the Request Financial Aid Info form is submitted.
- **Request a Visit**: visit confirmation, reminders, and an internal follow-up email to your staff after the visit.
- **Cold Inquiry Revival**: a series for older inquiries that went quiet. It starts when someone adds the Cold Inquiry Revival tag to a family.

Check the tone, your admissions steps, visit instructions and your signature. You don't need to rebuild anything.

**Visit Feedback Survey.** Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, and open Visit Feedback. It's the only survey that sends on its own, right after a visit is marked as attended and a good fit. Check the questions and wording, edit them or ask Banner 61 to, then approve it.

**Your info packets.** If you send families a parent info packet, tuition sheet or financial aid packet by hand today, send those files to the Banner 61 team. Banner 61 attaches them to the right automatic emails so they go out on their own.

## Sharing domain access

Sometimes Banner 61 needs access to your school's domain name (for example, to add the DNS records that let emails send from your school's address). Share access from your domain registrar, such as GoDaddy, Namecheap or Bluehost, with the Banner 61 team. Each registrar does this a little differently, so check its help pages or ask Banner 61.

ARTICLE: Contacts (id: contacts)

# Contacts

Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**. Every person your school interacts with is a contact: prospective parents, current families, alumni, donors and staff. Contacts are created when someone submits a form, books a visit, is added by hand or is imported.

## Find a contact

Use the search bar at the top of the screen, or open CRM, then Contacts and search or filter the list. Search before you add someone, so you don't create a duplicate.

## Add a contact by hand

Use this for phone calls, walk-ins and families you meet at events.

1. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and click **+ Add Contact**.
2. Enter first and last name, and an email or phone number (at least one is required).
3. Fill in any fields you know, like Grade(s) of Entry or student name.
4. Click **Save**.

Then make the record useful: add a tag (for example, Open House Attendee), add a note about why they're in the system, and, if they're a prospective family, create an opportunity so they show up in your pipeline. Capitalize names properly, because automatic emails greet families by name.

## What's on a contact record

- **Left panel (details)**: owner (the one staff member responsible), followers (others who get notified), tags, Do Not Disturb, and all fields. Click the filter icon and choose "Hide empty fields and folders" to show only what's filled in.
- **Center (conversation)**: every email, text, form submission and booking, newest at the bottom. You can send an email or text from here.
- **Right panel (actions)**: Activity (pages visited, forms submitted), Associations (link a parent to a spouse or student), Opportunities, Tasks, Notes, Appointments (book a visit directly), and Documents & Payments.

Notes are internal. Families never see them. Every entry on the timeline is time-stamped, so anyone on your team can pick up where someone else left off. Read it before you call a family so you don't repeat a question.

## Tags

Tags are labels on a contact. Use them to group families, filter lists and start automations.

- **Add a tag**: open the contact, click into **Tags**, type or pick the tag (choose Add New Tag if it doesn't exist), then click **Apply**. The tag only saves when you click Apply.
- **Add tags to many contacts**: in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, check the contacts, click **Add tags**, type an Action name, pick the tags and click **Add tags**. This only adds tags; it never removes existing ones. See [Tag every family on a list](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions/#tag-every-family-on-a-list).
- **Remove tags**: deselect the tag and click Apply, or select many contacts, open **More** and choose **Remove tags**. See [Remove a tag from a group of families](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions/#remove-a-tag-from-a-group-of-families).
- **Manage all tags**: go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Tags** to create, rename or delete tags.

Good to know:
- Tags are case-sensitive. "Tour Scheduled" and "tour scheduled" are two different tags. Pick one spelling.
- Tags belong to the contact, not the opportunity. A tag shows on every opportunity for that family.
- Renaming a tag does not update workflows, Smart Lists or filters that use it. Update those yourself, or ask Banner 61.
- Deleting a tag removes it from every contact. If that happens by mistake, an admin can restore it within 60 days from **Settings**, then **Audit Logs**.
- Some tags start automations. Adding **Cold Inquiry Revival** to a family starts the Cold Inquiry Revival emails. Ask before adding a tag you don't recognize.

Tags vs custom fields: a tag is a label (Tour Scheduled). A custom field holds a value (Grade Level Applying For = 5th Grade).

## Filter the contact list

Click **Filters** above the contact list in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, to narrow it to the families you need. To keep a filter as a list that updates on its own, save it as a smart list. Smart lists, including Current Families and Stalled Leads, have their own article: [Smart Lists](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/smart-lists/).

## Import contacts from a spreadsheet

1. Save your spreadsheet as a CSV file with a header row (First Name, Email, Grade and so on). Every row needs an email or phone number.
2. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and click **Import**.
3. Follow the steps and match each column to a field. Columns Banner Connect doesn't recognize show as Not mapped; pick the field from the dropdown, or check "Don't import data in unmapped columns" to skip them.

Tips: put general information in the Notes field, use the existing grade fields (Grade(s) of Interest, Grade(s) of Entry), and test with 5 to 10 rows before importing everyone. You can tag the whole import (for example, Open House March) to send them a follow-up. If you need a field that doesn't exist, ask Banner 61 to create it before you import.

## Export contacts

1. Filter the list or open a Smart List first, so the file only has who you need.
2. Check the contacts (the top checkbox selects everyone shown).
3. Click **Export** in the toolbar and confirm.
4. You get a notification when the file is ready. Download it from **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Bulk Actions**. Files are kept for 30 days.

The export includes the columns you can see, not full conversation history. Exported files contain private family information, so store them securely.

## Duplicates

- **Find duplicates**: in CRM, then Contacts, open the three-dot menu and choose **Manage Duplicates**. Match on email for the most accurate results.
- **Merge two contacts yourself**: check both contacts, click **More**, then **Merge**. Pick the record to keep (the most complete one, with the current email), choose which details win, and confirm.

Merging is permanent and can't be undone. Notes, tags, appointments, opportunities and conversations from both records are combined.

# The enrollment pipeline (Opportunities)

Go to **CRM**, then **Opportunities**. Each card is one family. Each column is a stage. Families move left to right as they get closer to enrolling.

## Your pipelines

Every account starts with these pipelines. Switch between them with the pipeline dropdown at the top left.

**Inquiry to Enrollment** (the main one):
1. New Inquiry: the family submitted a form or registered for an event.
2. Visit Scheduled: they booked a campus visit.
3. Visit Attended: they came to the visit.
4. Application Sent
5. Application Submitted
6. Accepted
7. Enrolled

**Re-Enrollment Pipeline**: Re-enrollment Offered, Intent Form Submitted, Contract Sent, Contract Signed, Tuition Paid, Re-enrolled.

**Sibling Enrollment Fast Track**: Sibling Intent Request, Application Sent, Application Submitted, Sibling Accepted, Sibling Enrolled.

Many moves happen on their own. A form submission creates a card in New Inquiry. Booking on the Schedule a Visit calendar moves the family to Visit Scheduled. Marking the visit as attended (from the follow-up email your staff receive) moves them to Visit Attended. The later stages you move by hand. For what each stage means and when to move a family, see [Your enrollment board, stage by stage](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/enrollment-stages/).

## What's on an opportunity

- **Contact**: the family it belongs to. Click the name to open the full contact record.
- **Pipeline and Stage**: where they are.
- **Status**: Open, Won, Lost or Abandoned (see [Status: Open, Won, Lost, Abandoned](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/pipeline/#status-open-won-lost-abandoned)).
- **Owner**: the staff member responsible for moving the family forward.
- **Followers**: up to 10 other staff who can see and edit the opportunity. Followers can't change the owner.
- **Value**: optional. Many schools leave it at $0; some use it for annual tuition.

The opportunity window also has **Notes**, **Tasks** and **Book/Update Appointment** in its left sidebar. Notes added here also show on the contact record. Notes are internal; families never see them.

## Add a family to the pipeline by hand

Any of these work:
- In **Opportunities**, click **+ Add Opportunity**, pick or create the contact, choose the pipeline and stage, and click **Create**.
- On a contact record, open the **Associations** icon (three connected dots) in the right panel, go to Opportunities and click **+ Add**.
- In **Conversations**, open the family's thread, click **Associations** on the right and add an opportunity.

## Move a family forward

Drag the card to the next column, or open the opportunity and change **Stage**. Move families as soon as something actually happens (for example, move to Visit Scheduled only after the visit is on the calendar) so the board always shows who's waiting on you.

## Status: Open, Won, Lost, Abandoned

- **Open**: still deciding. This is the default.
- **Won**: the family enrolled.
- **Lost**: they chose another school, weren't a fit, said they're not interested, or stopped responding after several tries.
- **Abandoned**: the opportunity went nowhere.

Marking a family Lost keeps their history and keeps your reports accurate. Use Lost instead of deleting when a family decides not to enroll.

The pipeline shows only Open opportunities by default. If you can't find a family, check the Status filter.

## Stalled families

When a family sits in the same stage of the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline for more than a week, Banner Connect tags them as stalled automatically. The tag comes off on its own when they move to the next stage. Use it as a prompt to follow up, move them forward or mark them Lost.

## Find a family

Type at least 3 letters into the search bar at the top of Opportunities. It matches the opportunity name, contact name, email, phone and tags, from the start of a word ("Thom" finds Thompson; "pson" doesn't). To search every pipeline at once, switch to list view and choose **All Pipelines**.

## Filters and views

- Click **Advanced filters** (the funnel icon) above the board to filter by stage, owner, status, tags, value or custom fields. Combine with AND or OR.
- Switch between the board and list view with the icons at the top right. List view can be sorted by name, value, date created or stage.
- Choose which details show on each card with **Manage Fields** at the top right of the board (up to 8).

Handy filters: Stage = Visit Attended (visited but no application yet); Owner = a counselor; Created Date = last 7 days.

## Update many families at once

1. Check the cards (hover a card and click the box in its corner) or rows in list view.
2. Click **Edit** to change Stage, Pipeline, Status, Owner, Followers, Value, Source, Expected Close Date, Lost Reason or custom fields for all of them.

For example, after an open house, filter to the families who attended, select them and move them to the next stage in one step. Bulk changes run in the background; check progress on the **Bulk Actions** page under Opportunities.

You can also move a group from a contact list in **CRM**, then **Contacts**. See [Update the pipeline for a whole list of families](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions/#update-the-pipeline-for-a-whole-list-of-families).

## Delete and restore

Delete only duplicates, test entries and records that don't belong. Deleting an opportunity doesn't delete the contact.

- **One**: open it, click the trash icon at the bottom of the window and confirm.
- **Several**: check them, click **Delete** and type DELETE to confirm.
- **Restore**: from the Bulk Actions page (three-dot menu, Restore), or from **Settings**, then **Audit Logs** (filter Action = Deleted, Module = Opportunity, then Restore). Only admins can restore, within 60 days, and only if the contact still exists.

## Changing pipelines and stages

Go to **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, then **Pipeline**. You can rename stages, reorder them, add or delete stages, and choose which stages show in dashboard charts. When you delete a stage, you pick where its families move, so no one is lost.

Be careful: the prebuilt automations move families into specific stages by name (for example, Visit Scheduled). Renaming or deleting those stages, or deleting a whole pipeline, can break them. Deleting a pipeline also deletes every opportunity in it. Ask the Banner 61 team before changing the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline.

# Conversations, snippets and texting

Go to **CRM**, then **Conversations**, then **Inbox**. Email, text messages, Facebook and Instagram messages and Google Business Profile messages all land in one list. Reply from the same screen, on your computer or in the LeadConnector mobile app.

## Connect Facebook, Instagram and Google

Someone at your school does this, because it signs in to the school's own accounts. It takes about ten minutes.

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect** next to Facebook and Instagram. Sign in with a Facebook account that's an admin on your school's page and choose the page. If your Instagram is a business account linked to that page, choose it too.
3. Click **Connect** next to Google Business Profile. Sign in with the Google account that manages your school's listing and choose your location.

Test it: send your school's Facebook page a message from your personal account. It should appear in Conversations within a minute.

## Reply to a family

Open the thread, choose the channel (email, SMS, Facebook and so on) in the message box, write your reply and send. Replies from families show up in the same thread. If you've connected your email (Settings, then My Profile, Email 2-way sync), emails also appear in your normal inbox.

## Snippets (saved replies)

Snippets are saved messages for questions you answer often, like tuition, uniforms or what to expect on a tour.

**Use a snippet:** in a Conversations thread, click the Snippets option in the message box, search or browse folders, click one to insert it, and edit before sending.

**Create a snippet:**
1. Go to **CRM**, then **Conversations**, then **Snippets**.
2. Click **New Snippet** and choose a text snippet (for texts) or an email snippet.
3. Name it by what it does ("Tour Reminder - 24 Hours"), add the content and save.

You can personalize snippets with merge fields like {{contact.first_name}}. To add a link, highlight the words, use the link button and paste the address or choose a trigger link. Organize snippets in folders such as Admissions - Inquiry Responses, Tours, Tuition and Financial Aid, and Current Families.

## Texting (SMS)

Texting needs a school phone number and carrier registration (called A2P 10DLC) before messages can send. Banner 61 handles this setup. If the SMS option isn't available in your account, ask the Banner 61 team.

Once texting is set up:
- **Text one family**: in Conversations, start a new conversation or open their thread, choose SMS, check the From number and send.
- **Text a group**: in CRM, then Contacts, filter and check the families, click the Send SMS icon, choose your school number, write the message (merge fields work), and send. Drip mode spreads large sends out over time.

Text during reasonable hours, keep messages short and friendly, use the family's first name, and avoid lots of links.

Phone numbers are managed in **Settings**, then **Communications**, then **Phone System**.

## Trigger links

A trigger link is a tracked link. When a family clicks it, the click is recorded on their record and it can start an automation (for example, send tuition details to everyone who clicks "View Tuition"). Find them in **CRM**, then **Conversations**, then **Trigger Links**, or under **Marketing**. See [Trigger links](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/marketing/#trigger-links) for how to create and use them.

ARTICLE: Tasks and notifications (id: tasks)

# Tasks and notifications

Tasks are internal to-dos with a due date and an owner: call a family back, send a tuition sheet, review an application. Families never see them.

## Where tasks live

- **CRM**, then **Tasks**: every task in the account. Filter to your own tasks, or by Due Today, Overdue or Upcoming.
- A contact record: the Tasks icon in the right panel.
- An opportunity: the Tasks section in its left sidebar.

A task created in one place shows up in the others.

## Create a task

1. Open the task list, a contact or an opportunity and click **+ Add Task**.
2. Enter a title that says what needs to happen ("Call the Smith family about the 2nd grade tour").
3. Add a description if it helps, a due date and time, and who it's **Assigned to**.
4. Turn on **Recurring** for work that repeats, like a weekly check of new inquiries.
5. Click **Save**.

Check a task off to complete it. Completed tasks stay on the family's record.

If a staff member isn't in the Assign to list, they need their own Banner Connect user first. Ask the Banner 61 team to add them.

## Turn on task notifications

Assigning a task doesn't alert anyone by itself. Each person chooses their own notifications:

1. Click **Settings**, then **My Profile**, then **Notifications**.
2. Open **Task Notifications**.
3. For each notification, check In-App, Email and/or SMS:
   - Notify when a task gets assigned to me
   - Reminder before an upcoming task (choose minutes, hours or days)
   - Daily summary of due tasks (choose the time)
   - Daily summary of overdue tasks (choose the time)
4. Click **Save** at the bottom right.

A good starting setup: assigned to me by In-App and Email; a reminder 1 hour before, In-App; both daily summaries at 8:00 AM by Email, with "Don't send notifications when there are no tasks" turned on.

Not getting notifications? Make sure you clicked Save, wait up to an hour for new reminders to start, check that the task is assigned to you with a due date, check the email and phone on your profile, and look in spam.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard shows enrollment at a glance: how many families reach each stage, the tuition still in the pipeline, and your tasks, with overdue ones in red. A good daily habit is to open it each morning and work your task list from the top.

# Calendars

The Calendars tab has four pages:
- **Calendar View**: everyone coming in, by day or week.
- **Appointment List**: every booking in a list you can search and filter.
- **Meeting Calendars**: your booking pages and their settings.
- **Connections**: link your Google, Outlook or iCloud calendar.

## Your prebuilt calendars

- **Schedule a Visit**: families book a campus visit. Booking here sends a confirmation and reminders automatically, and moves the family to Visit Scheduled in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline. After the visit, your staff get an email with links to mark what happened (see [After a visit: the staff follow-up email](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/automations/#after-a-visit-the-staff-follow-up-email)).
- **Event Registration**: for open houses, preview days and information nights. Families pick from the dates you add.
- **Schedule an Assessment**: turned off until your school needs it.

## Connect your calendar

Connecting your own calendar keeps online bookings from landing on top of things you already have.

1. Go to **Calendars**, then **Connections**.
2. Click to connect Google or Outlook and sign in with the account you use for appointments.
3. Choose which of your calendars Banner Connect should check for conflicts, so personal and school events block those times.
4. Choose the calendar new visits should be added to.

Then go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, open **Schedule a Visit**, and make sure the days and hours match when you actually give visits.

Tip: link your work calendar both ways so new visits appear there. Add a personal calendar as a conflict calendar only, so it blocks time without showing your private events. Each staff member can review their own calendar connections in **Settings**, then **My Profile**.

## Book a visit for a family (phone or walk-in)

1. Go to **Calendars**, then **Calendar View**, and make sure you're looking at Schedule a Visit.
2. Click a time slot, or click **+ New**.
3. Check that Calendar is set to **Schedule a Visit**.
4. Search for the family by name or email. If they're new, click **Add New Contact** and enter name, email and phone.
5. Confirm the date and time, add any notes (grade of interest, who referred them) and save.

The family gets the same confirmation and reminders as if they'd booked online, and moves to Visit Scheduled. This only happens on the Schedule a Visit calendar, so always check which calendar is selected.

## Change available times

Go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, and click the pencil next to the calendar. You can change the meeting length, the days and hours families can book, and add date-specific hours for holidays or event days. Under the advanced settings you'll also find the description families see, the location (your school address, a phone call or an online meeting), how far ahead families can book, minimum notice, buffers between appointments, daily limits, the booking form, and reminder and cancellation settings.

## Set up an event (open house)

Before you share the Event Registration calendar:

1. **Edit the description** so it matches your event.
2. **Add each event date**: go to **Calendars**, then **Meeting Calendars**, click the pencil next to Event Registration, go to **Availability**, click **Add Date Specific Hours**, pick the date, set the start and end time, and save. Repeat for each date.
3. **Confirm who gets the staff email**: go to **Automations**, open the **Event Registration** workflow, find the **Internal Staff Email** step, check the email address and save.
4. **Review the event emails** in **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**: Event Registration Confirmation Email #1, 24 Hours Before #2, 1 Hour Before #3, After Visit Internal Notification #4, Reschedule Visit Email #5, Thank You Email with Next Steps #6 and Visit Feedback Survey Email #7. Pay the most attention to #6, which tells families what to do next after the event.

When a family registers, Banner Connect adds them to New Inquiry (if they aren't in the pipeline yet), tags them Event Registration, sends a confirmation, a reminder 24 hours before and another 1 hour before. After the event your staff get an email with three links: they showed up (sends next steps and the feedback survey), they didn't show (sends a "sorry we missed you" email with a link to book a visit), or they showed but aren't a fit (no email). Click one per family.

## Email campaigns (one-time emails to a group)

Use a campaign for announcements, newsletters, event invitations and other one-time sends. It's the best way to email a group, because it looks polished and shows who opened and clicked.

Your account has a **Campaigns** template folder (**Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**) with four ready-to-edit starters: **Open House Invitation**, **Monthly Family Newsletter**, **Re-enrollment Is Open** and **Quick Announcement**. Each has your logo, signature and footer already in place. Replace every word in [BRACKETS] before you send.

1. Go to **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then the **Campaigns** tab, and create a new campaign.
2. Start from a template (one from the **Campaigns** folder is the quickest) or a blank layout, and build the email.
3. Click **Preview** to check desktop and mobile, and send a test to yourself.
4. Click **Send or Schedule** and fill in:
   - Sender name (such as "Admissions at Banner Christian School") and sender email. The sender email must use your school's verified sending address; ask Banner 61 if you're not sure which to use.
   - Subject line and preview text.
   - Recipients: individual contacts, tags, Smart Lists, a pre-built segment (like contacts who opened an email in the last 30 days), or a custom segment.
5. Optional: turn on **Track Clicks** before sending (you can't add it later), UTM tracking, or tagging families based on what they click.
6. Choose **Send Now**, **Schedule** (uses your account's time zone) or **Batch Schedule** for very large lists.

Attachments must total 10 MB or less. Families who unsubscribed or are set to Do Not Disturb are skipped automatically. To change a scheduled send, use the campaign's three-dot menu and choose Reschedule at least an hour before it goes out.

**Custom segments:** in the Recipients area choose **Build Segments**. Each rule has a field, a condition and a value (Tag is Prospective Family). **Add Filter** adds a rule that must also match (AND); **Add OR Filter** adds an alternative group (OR). Examples: Tag is Inquiry and Tag is not Tour Scheduled; Grade Level Applying For is Kindergarten. Save segments you'll reuse.

For a short, plain note to a list, you can also send straight from a contact list. See [Send a quick email to a list](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions/#send-a-quick-email-to-a-list).

**Results:** open the campaign's three-dot menu and choose **Statistics**, then **Load More Statistics** for clicks by link. For all email together, open the **Statistics** tab under Marketing, then Emails. Delivered means it reached the family's mail server, not that they opened it. Watch bounces, unsubscribes and spam complaints for list problems.

# Automations (workflows)

Go to **Automations**, then **Workflows**. Workflows are the follow-up that happens on its own. Banner 61 builds them for you. Before building anything new, look through what's already running.

## What's already built

| Workflow | Starts when | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Request Info Packet / Lead Nurture | A family submits the Request Info Packet form | Adds them to New Inquiry and sends a short series of follow-up emails |
| Request Tuition Info | The Request Tuition Info form is submitted | Sends your tuition information email |
| Request Financial Aid Info | The Request Financial Aid Info form is submitted | Sends your financial aid email |
| General Inquiry (contact form) | The General Inquiry form is submitted | Adds the family to New Inquiry and follows up |
| Request a Visit | A family books on the Schedule a Visit calendar (online or by staff) | Confirmation, reminders, moves them to Visit Scheduled, then emails your staff after the visit |
| Event Registration | A family registers on the Event Registration calendar | Confirmation, 24-hour and 1-hour reminders, staff email after the event |
| Cold Inquiry Revival | Someone adds the Cold Inquiry Revival tag | A series of re-engagement emails for older inquiries |
| Stalled Leads / Remove Stalled Tag | A family sits in one stage for over a week | Tags them as stalled, and removes the tag when they move |

Which ones are turned on depends on the forms and calendars your school uses. Ask Banner 61 if you're not sure.

## After a visit: the staff follow-up email

After each scheduled visit, your staff get an email with three links. Click the one that fits:
- **They showed and are a good fit**: moves the family to Visit Attended and sends the Visit Feedback Survey.
- **They showed but aren't a good fit**: records it, no email to the family.
- **They didn't show**: sends an email encouraging them to reschedule.

The link opens Banner Connect so you can check the result. The Event Registration staff email works the same way for events.

## Win back quiet families

To restart conversations with older inquiries who never booked a visit, add the **Cold Inquiry Revival** tag to the family (from their contact record, or from the pipeline). The Cold Inquiry Revival emails then send on their own. Review those templates first in **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**.

## How a workflow works

Every workflow has:
1. **A trigger**: what starts it, such as a form submitted, a visit booked, a tag added or a trigger link clicked.
2. **Filters** (optional): who it counts for, such as only the Request Info Packet form.
3. **Actions**: the steps, in order. Common ones are Wait, Send Email, Send Internal Notification (alert a staff member), Add Contact Tag, Add Task and Add to Workflow.

Each workflow also has a **Settings** tab with:
- **Allow Re-entry**: whether someone can go through it more than once.
- **Stop on Response**: removes a family from the workflow as soon as they reply to one of its emails or texts, so a person can take over.
- **Time window**: the hours messages are allowed to send.

**Save** keeps your changes. The **Draft/Publish** switch decides whether it runs; a workflow in Draft never sends anything.

## Before you change a workflow

Workflows send real emails to real families, and many of them depend on each other (for example, on exact stage names, tag names and templates). It's easy to send families duplicate or wrong emails by accident. Safe changes a school can make on its own:
- editing the wording of the email templates a workflow uses,
- changing the staff email address in a notification step (like Internal Staff Email in Event Registration).

To start a workflow by hand for a whole list of families, see [Start an automation for a list of families](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions/#start-an-automation-for-a-list-of-families).

For anything else, such as new workflows, new triggers, or turning a workflow on or off, ask the Banner 61 team. We'll build or change it with you.

## "Why didn't this send?"

Common reasons:
- The workflow is in **Draft**.
- The family doesn't match the trigger's filters (for example, they used a different form).
- They're set to Do Not Disturb or unsubscribed.
- **Stop on Response** removed them because they replied.
- They already went through it once and Allow Re-entry is off.

Each trigger has stats showing how many contacts were checked, matched and not matched in the last 30 days, with the reason for each one that didn't match. If you're stuck, send the family's name and the workflow name to the Banner 61 team.

# Advanced and settings

Most of what's under Advanced is set once by Banner 61 and rarely touched.

## Custom values (your school's details)

Go to **Advanced**, then **Custom Values**. These hold your school's name, address, phone number and similar details. Every email and page uses them, so a change here updates everything at once. Check that your school name, address and phone number are right. Example: the visit calendar uses {{custom_values.school_full_address}} as the visit location.

## Custom fields (extra details you track)

Go to **Advanced**, then **Custom Fields**, and choose Contact or Opportunity at the top.

- **Opportunity fields** describe one enrollment: Application Status, Admissions Priority, Enrollment Year. These can't go on forms.

**Create a field:** search first to make sure it doesn't exist, click **Create field**, choose the type (dropdowns keep answers consistent), set the name and folder, and save. Each field gets a **Key**, which is how emails insert it, like {{contact.grade_level_applying_for}}.

Be careful:
- You can't change a field's type, or move it between Contact and Opportunity, after it's created.
- Changing a field's Key breaks any email or text that uses the old one.
- Deleting a field permanently deletes its data from every record. There's no undo. Export first if you might need it, or move the field to an "Archive" folder instead.
- Fields can be used in forms, workflows and Smart Lists. Check those before changing or deleting a field.

Not sure whether you need a new field? Ask the Banner 61 team; we're happy to create it.

## Merge fields (personalizing messages)

Merge fields fill in each family's details when a message sends. Insert them with the merge field picker in the email or text editor rather than typing them, because they're case-sensitive.

- Contact: {{contact.first_name}}, {{contact.last_name}}, {{contact.email}}, {{contact.phone}}
- Appointment (only in visit confirmations and reminders): {{appointment.start_time}}, {{calendar.name}}
- Your school: {{location.name}}, {{location.phone}}, {{location.email}}
- Custom fields: {{contact.<field key>}}. Find the key in Advanced, then Custom Fields.

Empty fields come through blank. In emails, add fallback text to a merge field (click it in the editor), such as "there" so "Hi there," replaces "Hi ,". In texts, only use fields your forms always collect, like first name.

## Other Advanced pages

- **Media Storage**: logos and photos used in emails and forms.
- **Domains** and **Brand Boards**: your web addresses and brand colors. Banner 61 manages these.
- **Object Settings**: how records like Students are set up. Banner 61 manages this.

## Settings

- **My Profile**: your name, email, phone and password, your email connection (two-way sync), your calendar connections, and your notifications (including task notifications).
- **Users**: the people on your team. See [Users and access](#users-and-access) below.
- **Business Profile**: your school's name, logo, address, phone and time zone.
- **Communications**: phone numbers (Phone System), email sending and WhatsApp. Banner 61 sets these up.
- **Integrations**: connect Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile.
- **Audit Logs**: a record of who changed or deleted what, and where admins restore deleted opportunities and tags within 60 days.

## Users and access

- **Change someone's access**: click the edit icon next to their name.

# Smart Lists

A smart list is a saved filter of families that keeps itself up to date. Smart lists are the tabs across the top of **CRM**, then **Contacts**.

## What is a smart list?

A smart list is a saved search that shows whoever matches its rules today, so it never goes stale the way a spreadsheet tab does. You set the rules once, such as "families still deciding who haven't heard from us in two weeks." When a family books a visit, enrolls or inquires for the first time, the list changes on its own. Nobody has to move a family from one tab to another.

Your smart lists are the tabs across the top of **CRM**, then **Contacts**. A smart list never sends anything by itself. It only finds families, so you can act on them. To make your own, see [Make a smart list](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/smart-lists/#make-a-smart-list).

## What lists are already in my account?

Every account comes with these smart lists, as tabs across the top of **CRM**, then **Contacts**:

| List | Who's on it |
| --- | --- |
| All | Every contact in your account |
| Current Families | Families in the Enrolled stage |
| Stalled Leads | Families still deciding in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline who haven't had an email, text or call in the last 14 days |

To change which families a list shows, see [Make a smart list](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/smart-lists/#make-a-smart-list).

## Email or tag everyone on a list

Open the list, select the families, then choose the action. This is the quickest way to invite every family who toured to your open house, or to follow up with everyone who has gone quiet.

1. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and click the list's tab, such as **Stalled Leads**.
2. Check the families you want. The top checkbox selects everyone on the page.
3. Choose the action from the toolbar: send an email, add a tag, or move families to a new stage.

Check which tags you add before you click. Some tags start automatic emails: adding **Cold Inquiry Revival** starts the Cold Inquiry Revival emails to every family you selected. Ask the Banner 61 team before adding a tag you don't recognize.

How to check it worked: open one of the families and look for the email or tag on their record.

## Make a smart list

You can save any filter as its own list in about two minutes. Start from the **All** tab in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, so you don't change a list your team already uses.

1. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and click the **All** tab.
2. Click **Filters**.
3. Click **Add Filter**. Type in the search box to find a field, then pick it. For example, type "opportunity" to filter by pipeline, stage or status.
4. Choose the condition and the value, such as **Is** and **Visit Attended**.
5. To require a second rule as well, click **Add nested filter** inside the same box. A family must match every rule in one box.
6. Click **Apply** and look over the families that come back.
7. Click **Unsaved changes** at the top, then **Save as new smartlist**, and give it a name your team will recognize, like "Toured, Not Applied".

How to check it worked: the new list appears as a tab across the top of **CRM**, then **Contacts**.

Good to know:
- **Add nested filter** means "and": a family must match every rule in the box. **Add Filter** starts a new box, which means "or": a family can match either box.
- For dates like Last activity or Created, choose **In the last** and a number of days. The list then always counts back from today.
- Save a list only when your team will open it again and again. For a one-time question, like which families are interested in kindergarten, filter and then discard. See [Find families interested in one grade](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/smart-lists/#find-families-interested-in-one-grade).

Lists worth making:

| List | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| New Inquiries | Opportunity pipeline is Inquiry to Enrollment, stage is New Inquiry, and Opportunity status is Open |
| Toured, Not Applied | Same, with stage Visit Attended |
| Applications in Progress | Same, with stage Application Sent |
| New inquiries this week | Created, In the last 7 days, and stage is New Inquiry |

## Find families interested in one grade

Filter the **All** tab on Grade(s) of Entry. You don't need to save a list for this, so your tabs stay short. In **CRM**, then **Contacts**:

1. Click the **All** tab, then **Filters**, then **Add Filter**.
2. Type "grade" in the search box, pick **Grade(s) of Entry**, and set it to **Is** and the grade, such as **Kindergarten**. You can pick more than one grade.
3. Click **Apply**. Now you can email, tag or export the families shown.
4. When you're done, click **Unsaved changes**, then **Discard changes**, so the All tab goes back to showing everyone.

How to check it worked: the count at the top matches the families you expected, and each one shows that grade in Grade(s) of Entry on their record. Families whose grade isn't filled in won't appear, and a family with children in two grades shows up for both.

## Undo a change to a smart list

If you see **Unsaved changes** above a list, click it and choose **Discard changes**. That puts the list back the way it was, in **CRM**, then **Contacts**.

If you already clicked **Save**, the list changed for everyone on your team. Ask the Banner 61 team and we'll put it back.

How to check it worked: the **Unsaved changes** button is gone and the family count is back to what it was.

## Why is this family on Stalled Leads?

A family is on Stalled Leads because they're still deciding and nobody has emailed, texted or called them in the last 14 days. It doesn't mean they've lost interest. It means it's time for someone to reach out. The list is a tab in **CRM**, then **Contacts**.

A family is on the list when all three are true:
- They're in the **Inquiry to Enrollment** pipeline.
- Their status is **Open**: not enrolled and not marked Lost.
- There has been no email, text or call with them in the last 14 days.

Enrolled families and families marked Lost never appear. Updating a family's details, like their phone number or grade, doesn't take them off the list. Only an email, text or call does, including a reply from the family.

## Follow up with families on Stalled Leads

Work the list from the top, a few families at a time. Start with families furthest along in the pipeline, since a family at Application Sent is closer to enrolling than one at New Inquiry.

1. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and click the **Stalled Leads** tab.
2. Open a family and read their timeline, so you know where things left off.
3. Reach out by call, email or text, with a reason that fits where they are: a visit date, an application reminder, an answer to a question they asked.
4. If something has happened that the pipeline doesn't show, move them forward in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**.
5. If they've chosen another school or said they're not interested, mark them Lost. That keeps their history and keeps your reports accurate.

For older inquiries who never booked a visit, adding the **Cold Inquiry Revival** tag starts a short series of re-engagement emails. Review those emails first in **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then **Templates**.

How to check it worked: once you've emailed, texted or called a family, they drop off the Stalled Leads tab.

ARTICLE: Engagement score (id: engagement-score)
============================================================
# Engagement score

Every family in Banner Connect has an engagement score: a running number that goes up when they take a real step toward enrolling and comes down when they pull back. The rules that set the points live in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Engagement Score**.

## Decide which families to call first

A higher engagement score means a family has done more of the things that usually come before enrolling, so start your calls at the top. Use the score to plan your week, not to judge a family.

| Score | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 40 and up | They've visited, applied or both | Call them personally this week |
| 15 to 39 | They've asked for information or asked to visit | Make sure the visit happens, then follow up |
| 0 to 14 | Early interest, or they've pulled back | Keep them on your regular emails |

To see families in score order, go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, click **Sort**, choose **Engagement Score** and set it so the highest scores are on top.

The score only sees what happens in Banner Connect. A family who talked with you at a ball game or called the front office won't show it, so your own judgment still comes first.

## What adds points to a family's score

Points go up on their own each time a family does one of these things. Bigger steps toward enrolling are worth more. The rules live in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Engagement Score**.

| When a family... | Points |
|---|---|
| Is moved to the Application Submitted stage of the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline | +25 |
| Shows up for a visit, event or assessment (the appointment is marked Showed) | +15 |
| Fills out the Request a Visit form | +10 |
| Has the Application Fee field changed to Paid | +10 |
| Fills out the General Inquiry Form, Request Info Packet, Request Tuition Info or Request Financial Aid Info form | +5 |
| Fills out the Event Registration Form | +5 |
| Fills out the Visit Feedback survey | +5 |
| Replies to a message from your staff for the first time | +5 |
| Clicks a link in one of your emails | +2 |

A family who inquires, asks to visit, shows up and applies lands around 55.

Opening an email doesn't add points. Many phones now open every email automatically, so an "open" no longer means a parent read it. A click does.

How to check it worked: open a family who just did one of these things and confirm their score went up by the points in the table.

## Why a family's score went down

A family's engagement score drops when they miss a visit or tell you they don't want your emails, so the number reflects where they are now, not just their history. The rules live in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Engagement Score**.

| When a family... | Points |
|---|---|
| Is marked No-show for a visit, event or assessment | -5 |
| Unsubscribes from your emails | -10 |
| Marks one of your emails as spam | -20 |

A dropping score is a good reason to reach out personally. A short, warm check-in often restarts a stalled conversation.

## Why a family you know is serious has a low score

A low score usually means the family's interest happened outside Banner Connect. Phone calls, hallway conversations and paper applications don't add points. The score also only counts the Application Submitted stage when someone moves the family there in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**.

To bring their score in line, move them to the right stage in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline and book their next visit through the Schedule a Visit calendar.

How to check it worked: reopen the family's record and confirm the score went up.

## Change the points or add a rule

Check with the Banner 61 team before changing any scoring rule, because lists, reports and automations may depend on the current numbers. The rules live in **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Engagement Score**.

1. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Engagement Score**.
2. To change a rule, click the three dots at the end of its row, then **Edit**.
3. To add one, click **Add new rule**, choose what the family does, choose **Add** or **Subtract**, enter the points and click **Save**.

How to check it worked: the rule shows in the list with the points you entered.

ARTICLE: Your enrollment board, stage by stage (id: enrollment-stages)
============================================================
# Your enrollment board, stage by stage

Your enrollment board is the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**. Each card is one family, and each column is a step in your admissions process, from first inquiry to enrolled.

## What is the enrollment board for?

The enrollment board shows every family who is considering your school and how far along they are. It replaces the spreadsheet or whiteboard most admissions offices keep. A glance tells you how many families are waiting on a visit, an application or a decision, and who on your staff owns each one.

Find it in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, with Inquiry to Enrollment chosen in the pipeline dropdown at the top left. One card covers the whole family, even when they're enrolling more than one child. For how cards work in general, see [The enrollment pipeline](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/pipeline/).

## What does each stage mean?

Each stage in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline (**CRM**, then **Opportunities**) marks something the family has actually done:

1. **New Inquiry**: the family asked about your school, for example by submitting a form or registering for an event. Nothing else has happened yet.
2. **Visit Scheduled**: a campus visit or tour is on your calendar.
3. **Visit Attended**: the family came to the visit.
4. **Application Sent**: you sent the family the application, or told them how to apply.
5. **Application Submitted**: the family turned in their application.
6. **Accepted**: your school offered the family a place.
7. **Enrolled**: the family accepted and is enrolling. Mark the card Won at the same time.

A family who decides not to enroll stays in the stage they reached, with the status Lost. See [Mark a family who isn't enrolling](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/enrollment-stages/#mark-a-family-who-isnt-enrolling).

## Which moves happen on their own?

Three moves usually happen without anyone touching the board:

- A form submission or event registration creates a card in **New Inquiry**.
- Booking on the Schedule a Visit calendar moves the family to **Visit Scheduled**.
- Marking the visit as attended, from the follow-up email your staff receive, moves the family to **Visit Attended**.

From Application Sent on, your staff move the card by hand, because those steps usually happen outside Banner Connect (in your application system, by email or in person). Moving a family to Application Submitted also raises their engagement score. See [Engagement score](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/engagement-score/).

These moves are built into your account's automations. Check with the Banner 61 team before renaming or deleting a stage, because the automations look for the stages by name.

## Move a family to the next stage

Move a family as soon as the step actually happens. In **CRM**, then **Opportunities**:

1. Choose Inquiry to Enrollment in the pipeline dropdown at the top left.
2. Find the family's card. Type at least 3 letters of their name in the search bar if you need to.
3. Drag the card to the next column. Or click the card, change **Stage** and click **Update**.

When to move:
- To **Visit Scheduled** only when the visit is on the calendar, not when the family says they'll call back.
- To **Visit Attended** after they come, not when they book.
- To **Application Sent** when the family has the application in hand.
- To **Application Submitted** when you have the completed application.
- To **Accepted** when you've told the family they're accepted.
- To **Enrolled** when the family has committed, for example when the enrollment contract is signed or the deposit is paid. Set **Status** to Won at the same time.

How to check it worked: the card shows in the new column, and the "Days since last update" line on the card says Today.

## Why does it matter to keep the board up to date?

An up-to-date board is how your team knows who needs a call today. When cards sit in the wrong column, families who toured but never got an application slip through, and your counts for the head of school are wrong.

The board is also where your numbers come from. The dashboard and reports count families by stage, so a family who enrolled but still sits in Visit Attended makes your visits look like they don't turn into enrollments. To move many families at once, see [Update many families at once](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/pipeline/#update-many-families-at-once).

## Mark a family who isn't enrolling

When a family decides not to enroll, mark the card Lost instead of deleting it, so their history and your reports stay accurate. In **CRM**, then **Opportunities**:

1. Click the family's card.
2. Change **Status** to Lost.
3. Choose a **Lost reason**: chose another school, cost, moved, not a fit or no response.
4. Click **Update**.

Leave the card in the stage the family reached. That shows where in your process families drop off, for example after a visit or after acceptance.

How to check it worked: the card disappears from the open board. To see it again, change the Status filter at the top of the board to Lost or All.

If the Lost reason list is empty or missing a reason you need, ask the Banner 61 team to add it.

## Who owns each family on the board?

The owner is the staff member responsible for moving that family to the next step. The owner's initials show in the corner of each card. Most schools split families by division (for example, one person for PreK through 5th and another for 6th through 12th) or give every new inquiry to the admissions director.

To change the owner, click the card in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, choose a new **Owner** and click **Update**. To see only your families, use **Advanced filters** above the board and filter by Owner.

## What are the other two pipelines for?

Your account also has two shorter pipelines, chosen from the same dropdown in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**:

- **Re-Enrollment Pipeline**: current families moving through re-enrollment for next year. Stages: Re-enrollment Offered, Intent Form Submitted, Contract Sent, Contract Signed, Tuition Paid, Re-enrolled.
- **Sibling Enrollment Fast Track**: a current family enrolling a younger child. It skips the visit stages because the family already knows your school. Stages: Sibling Intent Request, Application Sent, Application Submitted, Sibling Accepted, Sibling Enrolled.

A current family can have a card in more than one pipeline at the same time, for example re-enrolling an older child while applying for a younger sibling. Check with the Banner 61 team before adding or renaming stages in any pipeline.

ARTICLE: How many families will enroll this fall? (id: enrollment-forecast)
============================================================
# How many families will enroll this fall?

The enrollment forecast estimates how many of the families on your enrollment board will enroll, how much tuition that brings in and which month they're likely to decide. Find it in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, then **Forecast**.

## What does the enrollment forecast show?

The enrollment forecast shows your best estimate of next year's new enrollment, based on where each family sits on your board today. It has two tabs in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, then **Forecast**:

- **Summary**: the totals. Expected revenue is the tuition you can expect from families still deciding. Won revenue is tuition from families already enrolled. Max potential revenue is what you'd collect if every open family enrolled.
- **Forecast timeline**: one column per month, holding the families expected to decide that month.

The forecast is an estimate, not a promise. It is most useful for spotting trends, such as fewer families reaching a visit than last year, well before the fall.

## Why does the forecast say "expected revenue"?

In Banner Connect, expected revenue means expected tuition. Each family's card is worth the tuition for the children they're enrolling, so a family enrolling two children at $8,500 each is worth $17,000. Expected revenue adds up every open family's tuition, each multiplied by that family's odds of enrolling.

For example, a family in Application Submitted worth $8,500 with 55% odds adds $4,675 to expected revenue. Read the total as "the tuition we can reasonably count on from families still deciding." See [How does Banner Connect estimate who will enroll?](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/enrollment-forecast/#how-does-banner-connect-estimate-who-will-enroll)

## How does Banner Connect estimate who will enroll?

Each stage of the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline (**CRM**, then **Opportunities**) carries the odds that a family in that stage will end up enrolling. Banner 61 starts every school with the same estimates:

| Stage | Starting odds |
|---|---|
| New Inquiry | 10% |
| Visit Scheduled | 30% |
| Visit Attended | 30% |
| Application Sent | 55% |
| Application Submitted | 55% |
| Accepted | 85% |
| Enrolled | 100% |

These starting odds are Banner 61's estimates for a typical Christian school, not research findings. Your own school's history is always better. See [Replace the starting odds with your school's numbers](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/enrollment-forecast/#replace-the-starting-odds-with-your-schools-numbers).

Expected new families is the number of open families in each stage times that stage's odds, added together. Twenty families in New Inquiry at 10% count as two expected families.

## How many seats will that fill?

Expected seats is your expected tuition divided by your tuition for one student. If expected revenue in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, then **Forecast**, then **Summary** is $105,400 and tuition is $8,500, expect about 12 more students. This works because each family's card is worth one tuition per child.

The Dashboard shows expected new families and expected seats as their own numbers, so you don't have to do the math. When your tuition differs by grade, or many families receive aid, treat expected seats as a rough count and check it against your grade-level lists.

## What is a close date for a school?

A close date is the date a family is expected to decide, usually your enrollment deadline for the school year they're applying for. A family applying for Fall 2027 might have a close date of March 1, 2027 if that's your priority deadline, or August 1, 2027 if you enroll all summer.

Banner Connect sets the close date for you when a family first comes in, based on the school year they're applying for. When a family is accepted, the close date moves to when you need their answer. The forecast timeline uses close dates to place each family in a month.

Check with the Banner 61 team before changing your enrollment deadlines in Banner Connect, because automations set close dates from them.

## Why is a family under "No close date"?

A family sits under No close date in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, then **Forecast**, then **Forecast timeline** when their card doesn't have a decision date yet. That usually means the card was added by hand or imported, so the automation that sets close dates never ran for it. The family still counts in expected revenue, but not in any month.

To fix one family, click the card, set **Close date** to your enrollment deadline for the year they're applying for and click **Update**. If many families show up here, ask the Banner 61 team to fill them in at once.

## Read your forecast before a board meeting

Open the forecast a few days before the meeting so you have time to clean up the board first. In **CRM**, then **Opportunities**:

1. Update the board first. Move any family whose stage has changed, and mark families who chose another school as Lost. See [Move a family to the next stage](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/enrollment-stages/#move-a-family-to-the-next-stage).
2. Click **Forecast**, then **Summary**, and choose Inquiry to Enrollment in the pipeline dropdown.
3. Note Won revenue (families already enrolled) and Expected revenue (families still deciding).
4. Divide each by your tuition for one student to turn them into students.
5. Click **Forecast timeline** to see which months families are expected to decide.

How to check it worked: the count of open families on the Summary tab matches the number of open cards on your enrollment board.

## Replace the starting odds with your school's numbers

Your own odds come from last year: of the families who reached each stage, how many enrolled. You can get the counts from last year's admissions spreadsheet, or from an enrollment report in your student information system.

1. For each stage, count the families who reached it last year, whether or not they went further.
2. Count how many of those families enrolled.
3. Divide enrolled by reached. If 40 families attended a visit and 14 of them enrolled, Visit Attended odds are 14 ÷ 40, or 35%.
4. Send your numbers to the Banner 61 team. They update the odds on your pipeline.

Small numbers swing a lot. If fewer than about 10 families reached a stage, combine two years or keep the starting estimate for that stage. Check with the Banner 61 team before changing odds yourself, because the Dashboard numbers use the same odds.

How to check it worked: in **CRM**, then **Opportunities**, then **Forecast**, then **Summary**, Expected revenue changes to reflect your new odds.

## Why do I see "Opportunity updated" lines in a conversation?

Those lines are the family's history, not messages. Banner Connect logs things like stage changes next to the conversation so you can see what happened and when. Families never see them.

To hide them, open the conversation, click the small speech-bubble menu at the top of the thread and choose **Conversations**. Choose **All** to bring them back.

## Answer common questions faster with snippets

Snippets are saved replies for questions you answer often, such as tuition, visits and financial aid. In the message box, open **Snippets**, pick one, change anything that's specific to this family and send. See [Snippets (saved replies)](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/conversations/#snippets-saved-replies).
