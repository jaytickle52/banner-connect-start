# Banner Connect Help Center: index

Official help for Banner Connect, the enrollment CRM Banner 61 sets up for Christian schools.
This is the only source of Banner Connect instructions. Menu names here are the real Banner Connect menu names.

How to use this index: find the one to three articles whose "Use when" or "Schools may call this" fits the question, then open their Read address. Each article is plain markdown, written so any one section makes sense on its own.
