Connect your school's Facebook and Instagram

Banner Connect help · Marketing > Social Planner · Reviewed 2026-11-16

Connecting your school's Facebook page and Instagram account in Marketing, then Social Planner, lets your staff write and schedule posts from Banner Connect. Someone at your school does this, because it signs in to the school's own accounts. Banner 61 can't connect them for you.

What do we need before we connect Facebook and Instagram?

You need three things, and it helps to check them before you start:

  • A Facebook page for the school, not a personal profile. Pages are the ones with a Like or Follow button.
  • A Facebook login that's an admin of that page. In Facebook, open the page and check its settings for who has access. If the person who set up the page left the school, ask them to add someone else first.
  • An Instagram business or creator account. Personal Instagram accounts can't be connected. In the Instagram app, open your profile, then Settings, and switch to a professional account; it's free and keeps your posts and followers. It works best when the Instagram account is linked to your school's Facebook page.

Whoever runs your school's social media is usually the right person to connect them. To do it, see Connect your school's Facebook page.

Connect your school's Facebook page

Connect Facebook first, from Marketing, then Social Planner, signed in as an admin of your school's page.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Social Planner. Until something is connected, it shows a list of accounts with a + Connect button under each.
  2. Click + Connect under Facebook.
  3. Sign in to Facebook with the login that's an admin of your school's page, and allow Banner Connect the access it asks for.
  4. Choose your school's page from the list and finish.

How to check it worked: your school's page shows as Connected on the Settings tab in Social Planner, and you can pick it under Post to in a new post. To write your first post, see Schedule a post to Facebook and Instagram.

Connect your school's Instagram account

Connect Instagram from Marketing, then Social Planner, after you've switched it to a business or creator account.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Social Planner.
  2. Click + Connect under Instagram.
  3. Sign in the way it asks. If your Instagram is linked to your school's Facebook page, sign in with the same Facebook login you used for the page. Otherwise, sign in with your school's Instagram username and password.
  4. Choose your school's Instagram account and finish.

How to check it worked: your Instagram account shows as connected in Social Planner, next to your Facebook page.

Ask someone else to connect your accounts

If someone outside the office manages your school's social media, send them an invite from Marketing, then Social Planner, so they can connect the accounts without a Banner Connect login and without sharing passwords.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Social Planner.
  2. Scroll down and click Send social connect invite.
  3. Enter their email and send it.

They open the email, sign in to Facebook and Instagram, and the accounts connect to your school's Banner Connect.

How to check it worked: after they finish, the accounts show as connected in Social Planner.

See which accounts are connected

Every connected page and account is listed in Marketing, then Social Planner, on the Settings tab.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Social Planner, and click Settings at the top right.
  2. Under Social accounts, check the list. Each one shows its type (for example Page), its status and how many days are left before it needs to be connected again.

To connect another account from this screen, click Connect Social.

How to check it worked: your school's page shows with the status Connected.

Why did our Facebook page disconnect?

Facebook connections expire after about two months, so Banner Connect has to be connected again from time to time. The Settings tab in Marketing, then Social Planner, shows how many days are left on each one. When it's close, or the status says Expired, click Connect Social and sign in again with the same Facebook login. To keep the connection from lapsing during a busy season, put a reminder on your calendar.

Why won't our Instagram account show up?

Almost always, the Instagram account is still a personal account, or the person connecting isn't an admin of the Facebook page it's linked to. Check these:

  • It's a personal Instagram account. Switch it to a business or creator account in the Instagram app, then try again.
  • The Facebook login isn't a page admin. Ask a current admin to give that person full access to the page in Facebook, then try again.
  • Instagram is linked to a different Facebook page, such as a church page or an old school page. Link it to the school's page in Facebook, or sign in with Instagram directly.
  • You chose only some pages or accounts when Facebook asked. Connect again and select the school's page and Instagram account.

If it still won't connect, send the Banner 61 team a screenshot of what you see.

Will parent messages from Facebook and Instagram come into Banner Connect?

Not from Social Planner alone. Connecting in Social Planner is only for posting; it doesn't bring messages into your inbox. To have messages from your Facebook page and Instagram land in your inbox with your email and texts, connect them in Settings, then Integrations as well. See Connect Facebook, Instagram and Google.
