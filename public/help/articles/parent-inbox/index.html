Getting started

One inbox for every parent message

Banner Connect help · CRM > Conversations > Inbox · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Every message a family sends lands in one place: CRM, then Conversations, then Inbox. Email, texts, Facebook and Instagram messages, Google Business Profile messages and website chats all appear in the same list, and the family's record sits beside the conversation so you can answer with the whole picture.

Where do parent messages show up?

They all show up in CRM, then Conversations, then Inbox, whichever way the family reached you. A small icon on each conversation tells you the channel: an envelope for email, the Facebook logo for Facebook, a speech bubble for website chat. Your reply goes back the same way unless you choose another channel in the message box.

Facebook, Instagram and Google messages only arrive once your school has connected those accounts. See Connect Facebook, Instagram and Google.

See what's new this morning

The Unread tab is your to-do list. It shows every conversation with a message nobody has opened yet, with the count beside it.

  1. Go to CRM, then Conversations, then Inbox.
  2. Click Team inbox in the narrow bar on the left to see every family's messages, or My inbox to see only the families assigned to you.
  3. Click the Unread tab. Work from the top down.

The other tabs: All shows every conversation, Recent shows the latest activity first and Starred shows conversations you've starred to come back to.

How to check it worked: once you've opened and answered a conversation, it drops off the Unread tab.

Hand a family to a colleague

A family belongs to whoever is set as their Owner. Change the owner and the conversation moves to that person's My inbox.

  1. Go to CRM, then Conversations, then Inbox, and open the family's conversation.
  2. In the Contact Details panel on the right, click the Owner box.
  3. Choose your colleague.
  4. If they need context, leave them a note. See Leave a note for staff.

How to check it worked: your colleague's name now shows under Owner, and the conversation appears when they click My inbox.

Leave a note for staff that the family won't see

An internal comment is a note inside the conversation that only your staff can see. Use it to pass on context, like "Mom mentioned a sibling for 2028."

  1. Go to CRM, then Conversations, then Inbox, and open the conversation.
  2. In the message box at the bottom, click the channel picker and choose Internal Comment.
  3. Type your note. To alert a colleague, type @ and their name.
  4. Send it.

How to check it worked: the note appears in the conversation in a different color, and nothing is sent to the family.

What is the panel beside each conversation?

The Contact Details panel on the right is the family's record, so you don't have to leave the inbox to look them up. It shows:

  • Owner: the staff member responsible for this family.
  • Tags: labels such as "interested" or "fee received."
  • Opportunities: the family's card in Inquiry to Enrollment and the stage they're in.
  • Workflows: any automations the family is in right now.
  • Engagement Score: how many steps toward enrolling the family has taken. See Decide which families to call first.

Use it before you reply. A family at Visit Attended with a high score needs a different answer than a brand new inquiry.

Why do I see "Opportunity updated" lines in a conversation?

Those lines are the family's history, not messages. Banner Connect logs things like stage changes next to the conversation so you can see what happened and when. Families never see them.

To hide them, open the conversation, click the small speech-bubble menu at the top of the thread and choose Conversations. Choose All to bring them back.

Answer common questions faster with snippets

Snippets are saved replies for questions you answer often, such as tuition, visits and financial aid. In the message box, open Snippets, pick one, change anything that's specific to this family and send. See Snippets (saved replies).
