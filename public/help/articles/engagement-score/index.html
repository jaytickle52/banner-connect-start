Engagement score

Banner Connect help · CRM > Contacts > Engagement Score · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Every family in Banner Connect has an engagement score: a running number that goes up when they take a real step toward enrolling and comes down when they pull back. The rules that set the points live in CRM, then Contacts, then Engagement Score.

Decide which families to call first

A higher engagement score means a family has done more of the things that usually come before enrolling, so start your calls at the top. Use the score to plan your week, not to judge a family.

Score What it usually means What to do
40 and up They've visited, applied or both Call them personally this week
15 to 39 They've asked for information or asked to visit Make sure the visit happens, then follow up
0 to 14 Early interest, or they've pulled back Keep them on your regular emails

To see families in score order, go to CRM, then Contacts, click Sort, choose Engagement Score and set it so the highest scores are on top.

The score only sees what happens in Banner Connect. A family who talked with you at a ball game or called the front office won't show it, so your own judgment still comes first.

What adds points to a family's score

Points go up on their own each time a family does one of these things. Bigger steps toward enrolling are worth more. The rules live in CRM, then Contacts, then Engagement Score.

When a family... Points
Is moved to the Application Submitted stage of the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline +25
Shows up for a visit, event or assessment (the appointment is marked Showed) +15
Fills out the Request a Visit form +10
Has the Application Fee field changed to Paid +10
Fills out the General Inquiry Form, Request Info Packet, Request Tuition Info or Request Financial Aid Info form +5
Fills out the Event Registration Form +5
Fills out the Visit Feedback survey +5
Replies to a message from your staff for the first time +5
Clicks a link in one of your emails +2

A family who inquires, asks to visit, shows up and applies lands around 55.

Opening an email doesn't add points. Many phones now open every email automatically, so an "open" no longer means a parent read it. A click does.

How to check it worked: open a family who just did one of these things and confirm their score went up by the points in the table.

Why a family's score went down

A family's engagement score drops when they miss a visit or tell you they don't want your emails, so the number reflects where they are now, not just their history. The rules live in CRM, then Contacts, then Engagement Score.

When a family... Points
Is marked No-show for a visit, event or assessment -5
Unsubscribes from your emails -10
Marks one of your emails as spam -20

A dropping score is a good reason to reach out personally. A short, warm check-in often restarts a stalled conversation.

Why a family you know is serious has a low score

A low score usually means the family's interest happened outside Banner Connect. Phone calls, hallway conversations and paper applications don't add points. The score also only counts the Application Submitted stage when someone moves the family there in CRM, then Opportunities.

To bring their score in line, move them to the right stage in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline and book their next visit through the Schedule a Visit calendar.

How to check it worked: reopen the family's record and confirm the score went up.

Change the points or add a rule

Check with the Banner 61 team before changing any scoring rule, because lists, reports and automations may depend on the current numbers. The rules live in CRM, then Contacts, then Engagement Score.

  1. Go to CRM, then Contacts, then Engagement Score.
  2. To change a rule, click the three dots at the end of its row, then Edit.
  3. To add one, click Add new rule, choose what the family does, choose Add or Subtract, enter the points and click Save.

How to check it worked: the rule shows in the list with the points you entered.
