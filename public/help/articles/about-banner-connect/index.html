About Banner Connect

Banner Connect help · All tabs · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Banner Connect is the enrollment CRM Banner 61 sets up for Christian schools. It keeps every family, message, visit and follow-up in one place, from the first inquiry to an enrolled student. Banner 61 builds each school's account with forms, a pipeline, calendars, email templates and automations already in place, so most schools start by reviewing what's there rather than building from scratch.

Sign in at crm.banner61.com. On a phone, use the LeadConnector app (search for it in the App Store or Google Play) with the same email and password.

Words Banner Connect uses

  • Contact: one person, usually a parent. Every form, email and booking connects to a contact.
  • Opportunity: one family's place in your enrollment process. It's the card you see on the pipeline board.
  • Pipeline: the columns a family moves through, such as New Inquiry, Visit Scheduled and Enrolled.
  • Stage: one column in a pipeline.
  • Status: Open, Won (enrolled), Lost or Abandoned. Status is separate from stage.
  • Tag: a label on a contact, such as Event Registration or Cold Inquiry Revival. Tags can start automations.
  • Smart List: a saved filter of contacts that updates on its own.
  • Workflow: an automation. It starts when something happens (a form is submitted, a visit is booked) and then sends emails, adds tags, creates tasks and so on.
  • Template: a saved email that workflows and campaigns reuse.
  • Snippet: a saved reply you can drop into a message.
  • Custom field: an extra detail you track, like Grade(s) of Entry.
  • Custom value: a school detail used in every email, like your school's name, address or phone number.

Names in your account

Banner 61 sets up every account with the same names. Use them exactly, because automations depend on them.

  • Forms (Forms & Docs, then Forms): General Inquiry Form, Request Info Packet, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info, Request a Visit, Event Registration Form, Sibling Intent Form. See Your prebuilt forms.
  • Calendars (Calendars, then Meeting Calendars): Schedule a Visit (campus visits), Event Registration (open houses and events), Schedule an Assessment (off until needed). See Your prebuilt calendars.
  • Pipelines (CRM, then Opportunities): Inquiry to Enrollment (New Inquiry, Visit Scheduled, Visit Attended, Application Sent, Application Submitted, Accepted, Enrolled), Re-Enrollment Pipeline, Sibling Enrollment Fast Track. See Your pipelines.
  • Smart lists (CRM, then Contacts): Current Families (everyone in the Enrolled stage) and Stalled Leads (open families in Inquiry to Enrollment with no email, text or call in 14 days). See Smart Lists.
  • Workflows (Automations, then Workflows): Request Info Packet / Lead Nurture, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info, General Inquiry, Request a Visit, Event Registration, Cold Inquiry Revival, Stalled Leads / Remove Stalled Tag, Engagement score: application submitted. See What's already built.
  • Email templates (Marketing, then Emails, then Templates): one folder per workflow above, plus a Campaigns folder with Open House Invitation, Monthly Family Newsletter, Re-enrollment Is Open and Quick Announcement for one-time sends. See Email campaigns.
  • Lost reasons (on an opportunity, when Status is Lost): Chose another school, Cost, Moved, Not a fit, No response. See Mark a family who isn't enrolling.
  • Tags that start automations: Cold Inquiry Revival (a series of re-engagement emails). Check before adding any tag.

What Banner 61 handles

Banner 61 sets up the account, the prebuilt forms, workflows, templates and pipeline. Banner 61 can also add your inquiry form to your website, create new custom fields, attach your info packets to emails, add users, and change account-level settings. When a school isn't sure whether something is safe to change, the answer is to ask the Banner 61 team first.

Some things only the school can do, because they sign in to the school's own accounts: connecting Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile, connecting a staff member's email or calendar, and connecting a payment account.
