The Forms & Docs tab has Forms, Surveys, and Documents & Contracts.

## Your prebuilt forms

- **General Inquiry Form (contact form)**: a simple contact form.
- **Request Info Packet**: the main inquiry form. Families who submit it get the Request Info Packet / Lead Nurture emails.
- **Request Tuition Info** and **Request Financial Aid Info**: each sends its own information email.
- **Request a Visit**: a family asks you to contact them about a visit.
- **Event Registration Form**: used by the Event Registration calendar.
- **Sibling Intent Form**: for current families adding a sibling.

Every submission creates or updates the family's contact record, adds to their engagement score and starts that form's follow-up if your school has it turned on. You can see every submission in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, on the **Submissions** tab. See [What happens when a family fills out a form](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/form-submissions/).

## Put your inquiry form on your website

You copy the form's embed code from **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms** (open the form, then click **Integrate**), and paste it in a code or HTML block on your website. For the full steps for WordPress, Squarespace, Wix and other builders, which form to use on which page, and how to test it, see [Put your inquiry form on your website](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/website-forms/). If you'd rather not do it yourself, share access to your website with Banner 61 and we'll add it.

## Edit a form

Open the form in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**.
- **Add a field**: drag one in from the elements panel. Use **Quick Add** for a brand-new field, or **Add Object Fields** to reuse a field that already exists in your account.
- **Change a field**: click it to change the label, placeholder text or whether it's required (or click the gear icon).
- **Reorder**: drag fields into place.
- **Style**: click the sliders icon at the top right to open Styles, Themes and Advanced (custom CSS). You can use one or two columns, set field widths, and adjust colors and fonts for your brand.
- **Show or hide questions based on answers**: click the branching icon in the top toolbar (Conditional Logic). You can show or hide fields, show a custom message, send families to a page after they submit, or disqualify a submission.
- **Email alerts and auto-reply**: click the bell icon to send your team an email on each submission, or an automatic reply to the family.
- Click **Save Form** when you're done.

Good to know:
- Reuse existing fields. If several forms ask "How did you hear about us?", use the same field on each so answers land in one place. If you see "A custom field already uses this name", remove the new field and add the existing one from Add Object Fields instead.
- Your prebuilt forms already send automatic emails through workflows. Turning on the form's own auto-responder too may send families two replies.
- Changing a form that's live on your website changes it for families right away.

## Surveys

Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**. The **Visit Feedback** survey sends automatically after a visit is marked as attended and a good fit. Review its questions and wording before your visits start. You can edit any survey yourself, or ask Banner 61 to.

## Documents & Contracts

Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Documents & Contracts**. Use it for enrollment agreements, permission slips, consent forms, waivers and handbook acknowledgments that families sign online.

**Send a document to one family:** create or open a template (your account includes an **Enrollment Agreement** template), send it to the family's contact, and they sign by email. Signed copies show under Completed.

**Share a public document with anyone:**
1. Go to Templates, click **New**, then **Create Public Document**.
2. Add fields: text, dates, checkboxes, dropdowns, signatures and initials.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Generate Links**, and copy the link.
4. Share it by email, text, on your website or as a QR code.

Signers don't need an account. They enter their name and email, fill in the fields, sign with a mouse or finger, and get a signed copy by email. One link works for any number of people until you unpublish it.

- **See who's signed**: open Documents & Contracts and check the responses or Completed list. Use the three-dot menu to download a signed PDF.
- **Stop accepting signatures**: unpublish the document. To edit it, unpublish, make changes and publish again.
- **Reuse it next year**: duplicate the template and rename it (for example, "2026-27 Field Trip Waiver – Grade 5").
- **Get notified when someone signs**: this needs a small workflow (Documents & Contracts trigger, status Signed, then an internal notification). Ask Banner 61 if you'd like one set up.
