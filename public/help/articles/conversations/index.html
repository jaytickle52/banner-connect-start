Getting started

Conversations, snippets and texting

Banner Connect help · CRM > Conversations · Reviewed 2026-11-03

Go to CRM, then Conversations, then Inbox. Email, text messages, Facebook and Instagram messages and Google Business Profile messages all land in one list. Reply from the same screen, on your computer or in the LeadConnector mobile app.

Connect Facebook, Instagram and Google

Someone at your school does this, because it signs in to the school's own accounts. It takes about ten minutes.

  1. Go to Settings, then Integrations.
  2. Click Connect next to Facebook and Instagram. Sign in with a Facebook account that's an admin on your school's page and choose the page. If your Instagram is a business account linked to that page, choose it too.
  3. Click Connect next to Google Business Profile. Sign in with the Google account that manages your school's listing and choose your location.

Test it: send your school's Facebook page a message from your personal account. It should appear in Conversations within a minute.

Reply to a family

Open the thread, choose the channel (email, SMS, Facebook and so on) in the message box, write your reply and send. Replies from families show up in the same thread. If you've connected your email (Settings, then My Profile, Email 2-way sync), emails also appear in your normal inbox.

Snippets (saved replies)

Snippets are saved messages for questions you answer often, like tuition, uniforms or what to expect on a tour.

Use a snippet: in a Conversations thread, click the Snippets option in the message box, search or browse folders, click one to insert it, and edit before sending.

Create a snippet: 1. Go to CRM, then Conversations, then Snippets. 2. Click New Snippet and choose a text snippet (for texts) or an email snippet. 3. Name it by what it does ("Tour Reminder - 24 Hours"), add the content and save.

You can personalize snippets with merge fields like {{contact.first_name}}. To add a link, highlight the words, use the link button and paste the address or choose a trigger link. Organize snippets in folders such as Admissions - Inquiry Responses, Tours, Tuition and Financial Aid, and Current Families.

Texting (SMS)

Texting needs a school phone number and carrier registration (called A2P 10DLC) before messages can send. Banner 61 handles this setup. If the SMS option isn't available in your account, ask the Banner 61 team.

Once texting is set up: - Text one family: in Conversations, start a new conversation or open their thread, choose SMS, check the From number and send. - Text a group: in CRM, then Contacts, filter and check the families, click the Send SMS icon, choose your school number, write the message (merge fields work), and send. Drip mode spreads large sends out over time.

Text during reasonable hours, keep messages short and friendly, use the family's first name, and avoid lots of links.

Phone numbers are managed in Settings, then Communications, then Phone System.

Trigger links

A trigger link is a tracked link. When a family clicks it, the click is recorded on their record and it can start an automation (for example, send tuition details to everyone who clicks "View Tuition"). Find them in CRM, then Conversations, then Trigger Links, or under Marketing. See Trigger links for how to create and use them.
