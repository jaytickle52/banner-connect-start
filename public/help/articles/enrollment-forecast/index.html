How many families will enroll this fall?

Banner Connect help · CRM > Opportunities > Forecast · Reviewed 2026-11-13

The enrollment forecast estimates how many of the families on your enrollment board will enroll, how much tuition that brings in and which month they're likely to decide. Find it in CRM, then Opportunities, then Forecast.

What does the enrollment forecast show?

The enrollment forecast shows your best estimate of next year's new enrollment, based on where each family sits on your board today. It has two tabs in CRM, then Opportunities, then Forecast:

  • Summary: the totals. Expected revenue is the tuition you can expect from families still deciding. Won revenue is tuition from families already enrolled. Max potential revenue is what you'd collect if every open family enrolled.
  • Forecast timeline: one column per month, holding the families expected to decide that month.

The forecast is an estimate, not a promise. It is most useful for spotting trends, such as fewer families reaching a visit than last year, well before the fall.

Why does the forecast say "expected revenue"?

In Banner Connect, expected revenue means expected tuition. Each family's card is worth the tuition for the children they're enrolling, so a family enrolling two children at $8,500 each is worth $17,000. Expected revenue adds up every open family's tuition, each multiplied by that family's odds of enrolling.

For example, a family in Application Submitted worth $8,500 with 55% odds adds $4,675 to expected revenue. Read the total as "the tuition we can reasonably count on from families still deciding." See How does Banner Connect estimate who will enroll?

How does Banner Connect estimate who will enroll?

Each stage of the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline (CRM, then Opportunities) carries the odds that a family in that stage will end up enrolling. Banner 61 starts every school with the same estimates:

Stage Starting odds
New Inquiry 10%
Visit Scheduled 30%
Visit Attended 30%
Application Sent 55%
Application Submitted 55%
Accepted 85%
Enrolled 100%

These starting odds are Banner 61's estimates for a typical Christian school, not research findings. Your own school's history is always better. See Replace the starting odds with your school's numbers.

Expected new families is the number of open families in each stage times that stage's odds, added together. Twenty families in New Inquiry at 10% count as two expected families.

How many seats will that fill?

Expected seats is your expected tuition divided by your tuition for one student. If expected revenue in CRM, then Opportunities, then Forecast, then Summary is $105,400 and tuition is $8,500, expect about 12 more students. This works because each family's card is worth one tuition per child.

The Dashboard shows expected new families and expected seats as their own numbers, so you don't have to do the math. When your tuition differs by grade, or many families receive aid, treat expected seats as a rough count and check it against your grade-level lists.

What is a close date for a school?

A close date is the date a family is expected to decide, usually your enrollment deadline for the school year they're applying for. A family applying for Fall 2027 might have a close date of March 1, 2027 if that's your priority deadline, or August 1, 2027 if you enroll all summer.

Banner Connect sets the close date for you when a family first comes in, based on the school year they're applying for. When a family is accepted, the close date moves to when you need their answer. The forecast timeline uses close dates to place each family in a month.

Check with the Banner 61 team before changing your enrollment deadlines in Banner Connect, because automations set close dates from them.

Why is a family under "No close date"?

A family sits under No close date in CRM, then Opportunities, then Forecast, then Forecast timeline when their card doesn't have a decision date yet. That usually means the card was added by hand or imported, so the automation that sets close dates never ran for it. The family still counts in expected revenue, but not in any month.

To fix one family, click the card, set Close date to your enrollment deadline for the year they're applying for and click Update. If many families show up here, ask the Banner 61 team to fill them in at once.

Read your forecast before a board meeting

Open the forecast a few days before the meeting so you have time to clean up the board first. In CRM, then Opportunities:

  1. Update the board first. Move any family whose stage has changed, and mark families who chose another school as Lost. See Move a family to the next stage.
  2. Click Forecast, then Summary, and choose Inquiry to Enrollment in the pipeline dropdown.
  3. Note Won revenue (families already enrolled) and Expected revenue (families still deciding).
  4. Divide each by your tuition for one student to turn them into students.
  5. Click Forecast timeline to see which months families are expected to decide.

How to check it worked: the count of open families on the Summary tab matches the number of open cards on your enrollment board.

Replace the starting odds with your school's numbers

Your own odds come from last year: of the families who reached each stage, how many enrolled. You can get the counts from last year's admissions spreadsheet, or from an enrollment report in your student information system.

  1. For each stage, count the families who reached it last year, whether or not they went further.
  2. Count how many of those families enrolled.
  3. Divide enrolled by reached. If 40 families attended a visit and 14 of them enrolled, Visit Attended odds are 14 ÷ 40, or 35%.
  4. Send your numbers to the Banner 61 team. They update the odds on your pipeline.

Small numbers swing a lot. If fewer than about 10 families reached a stage, combine two years or keep the starting estimate for that stage. Check with the Banner 61 team before changing odds yourself, because the Dashboard numbers use the same odds.

How to check it worked: in CRM, then Opportunities, then Forecast, then Summary, Expected revenue changes to reflect your new odds.
