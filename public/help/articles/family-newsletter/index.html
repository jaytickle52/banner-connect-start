Getting started

Send your monthly family newsletter

Banner Connect help · Marketing > Emails · Reviewed 2026-11-16

A monthly newsletter is an email campaign sent to your current families. Your account has a Monthly Family Newsletter template in Marketing, then Emails, then Templates, in the Campaigns (you send these) folder, so most of the work is filling in this month's news.

Who should get our family newsletter?

Send it to the Current Families smart list. That list holds every family in the Enrolled stage of your Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline, and it updates on its own as families enroll, so you never have to rebuild it.

Prospective families who are still deciding usually get their own emails from your automations. If you want them to see the newsletter too, add a second group when you pick recipients, for example everyone with an open opportunity, and check the list with the Banner 61 team the first time. To send it, see Send your monthly family newsletter.

Send your monthly family newsletter

Start a campaign from the Monthly Family Newsletter template in Marketing, then Emails, fill in this month's news, and send it to Current Families.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Emails, then the Campaigns tab.
  2. Click Create campaign, choose Your Templates, and pick Monthly Family Newsletter.
  3. Click the pencil next to the name at the top and rename it for the month, for example "December Family Newsletter".
  4. Replace every word in [BRACKETS] with this month's news: one story or student win, the dates coming up, and one way families can help or pray. Delete any section you don't need.
  5. Click the eye icon at the top to preview it on a computer and a phone. Check that the logo at the top is your school's.
  6. Click Send or schedule and fill in the sender name (for example "Banner Christian School"), the sender email your school sends from, the subject line and the preview text. If you're not sure which sender email to use, ask the Banner 61 team.
  7. Under recipients, choose the Current Families smart list.
  8. Send yourself a test first. When it looks right, choose Send now or schedule it (see Schedule the newsletter ahead of time).

The campaign saves as you work, so you can stop and come back to it from the Campaigns tab.

How to check it worked: the campaign shows on the Campaigns tab with its send date (or as Scheduled), and your own test copy looks right on your phone.

Schedule the newsletter ahead of time

You can write the newsletter now and have it go out later, from Marketing, then Emails.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Emails, then the Campaigns tab, and open the newsletter.
  2. Click Send or schedule and fill in the sender, subject and recipients.
  3. Choose Schedule and pick the date and time. It uses your school's time zone.

To change the time later, open the campaign's three-dot menu on the Campaigns tab and choose Reschedule at least an hour before it goes out.

How to check it worked: the campaign shows as Scheduled on the Campaigns tab with the date you picked.

See who opened the newsletter

Each campaign has its own results in Marketing, then Emails, then the Campaigns tab.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Emails, then the Campaigns tab.
  2. Click the newsletter to see how many were sent, delivered, opened and clicked.
  3. For every email your school has sent together, open the Statistics tab.

Delivered means the email reached the family's email provider, not that they read it. If a lot of emails bounce, the email addresses on those families are probably wrong; fix them on each contact in CRM, then Contacts.

How to check it worked: the numbers show a day or two after it sends, once families have had time to open it.

Why didn't some families get the newsletter?

Banner Connect skips anyone it can't or shouldn't email, so a few families on the list may not get it. The usual reasons:

  • They unsubscribed from your emails, or their contact is set to Do Not Disturb.
  • There's no email address on their contact, or the address is misspelled.
  • They aren't on the Current Families list, because their family isn't in the Enrolled stage yet. See Move a family forward.
  • It went to spam or Promotions. Ask the family to check there and mark it as not spam.

Open the family in CRM, then Contacts, to check their email and Do Not Disturb settings. Don't turn Do Not Disturb off for a family who asked not to be emailed. If many families aren't getting your emails, ask the Banner 61 team to check your school's email sending setup.
