Your enrollment board, stage by stage

Banner Connect help · CRM > Opportunities · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Your enrollment board is the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline in CRM, then Opportunities. Each card is one family, and each column is a step in your admissions process, from first inquiry to enrolled.

What is the enrollment board for?

The enrollment board shows every family who is considering your school and how far along they are. It replaces the spreadsheet or whiteboard most admissions offices keep. A glance tells you how many families are waiting on a visit, an application or a decision, and who on your staff owns each one.

Find it in CRM, then Opportunities, with Inquiry to Enrollment chosen in the pipeline dropdown at the top left. One card covers the whole family, even when they're enrolling more than one child. For how cards work in general, see The enrollment pipeline.

What does each stage mean?

Each stage in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline (CRM, then Opportunities) marks something the family has actually done:

  1. New Inquiry: the family asked about your school, for example by submitting a form or registering for an event. Nothing else has happened yet.
  2. Visit Scheduled: a campus visit or tour is on your calendar.
  3. Visit Attended: the family came to the visit.
  4. Application Sent: you sent the family the application, or told them how to apply.
  5. Application Submitted: the family turned in their application.
  6. Accepted: your school offered the family a place.
  7. Enrolled: the family accepted and is enrolling. Mark the card Won at the same time.

A family who decides not to enroll stays in the stage they reached, with the status Lost. See Mark a family who isn't enrolling.

Which moves happen on their own?

Three moves usually happen without anyone touching the board:

  • A form submission or event registration creates a card in New Inquiry.
  • Booking on the Schedule a Visit calendar moves the family to Visit Scheduled.
  • Marking the visit as attended, from the follow-up email your staff receive, moves the family to Visit Attended.

From Application Sent on, your staff move the card by hand, because those steps usually happen outside Banner Connect (in your application system, by email or in person). Moving a family to Application Submitted also raises their engagement score. See Engagement score.

These moves are built into your account's automations. Check with the Banner 61 team before renaming or deleting a stage, because the automations look for the stages by name.

Move a family to the next stage

Move a family as soon as the step actually happens. In CRM, then Opportunities:

  1. Choose Inquiry to Enrollment in the pipeline dropdown at the top left.
  2. Find the family's card. Type at least 3 letters of their name in the search bar if you need to.
  3. Drag the card to the next column. Or click the card, change Stage and click Update.

When to move: - To Visit Scheduled only when the visit is on the calendar, not when the family says they'll call back. - To Visit Attended after they come, not when they book. - To Application Sent when the family has the application in hand. - To Application Submitted when you have the completed application. - To Accepted when you've told the family they're accepted. - To Enrolled when the family has committed, for example when the enrollment contract is signed or the deposit is paid. Set Status to Won at the same time.

How to check it worked: the card shows in the new column, and the "Days since last update" line on the card says Today.

Why does it matter to keep the board up to date?

An up-to-date board is how your team knows who needs a call today. When cards sit in the wrong column, families who toured but never got an application slip through, and your counts for the head of school are wrong.

The board is also where your numbers come from. The dashboard and reports count families by stage, so a family who enrolled but still sits in Visit Attended makes your visits look like they don't turn into enrollments. To move many families at once, see Update many families at once.

Mark a family who isn't enrolling

When a family decides not to enroll, mark the card Lost instead of deleting it, so their history and your reports stay accurate. In CRM, then Opportunities:

  1. Click the family's card.
  2. Change Status to Lost.
  3. Choose a Lost reason: chose another school, cost, moved, not a fit or no response.
  4. Click Update.

Leave the card in the stage the family reached. That shows where in your process families drop off, for example after a visit or after acceptance.

How to check it worked: the card disappears from the open board. To see it again, change the Status filter at the top of the board to Lost or All.

If the Lost reason list is empty or missing a reason you need, ask the Banner 61 team to add it.

Who owns each family on the board?

The owner is the staff member responsible for moving that family to the next step. The owner's initials show in the corner of each card. Most schools split families by division (for example, one person for PreK through 5th and another for 6th through 12th) or give every new inquiry to the admissions director.

To change the owner, click the card in CRM, then Opportunities, choose a new Owner and click Update. To see only your families, use Advanced filters above the board and filter by Owner.

What are the other two pipelines for?

Your account also has two shorter pipelines, chosen from the same dropdown in CRM, then Opportunities:

  • Re-Enrollment Pipeline: current families moving through re-enrollment for next year. Stages: Re-enrollment Offered, Intent Form Submitted, Contract Sent, Contract Signed, Tuition Paid, Re-enrolled.
  • Sibling Enrollment Fast Track: a current family enrolling a younger child. It skips the visit stages because the family already knows your school. Stages: Sibling Intent Request, Application Sent, Application Submitted, Sibling Accepted, Sibling Enrolled.

A current family can have a card in more than one pipeline at the same time, for example re-enrolling an older child while applying for a younger sibling. Check with the Banner 61 team before adding or renaming stages in any pipeline.
