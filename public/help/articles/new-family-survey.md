# Hear from new families after their first 60 days

Menu: Forms & Docs > Surveys
Use when: Asking families in their first year how the start went: when to send the New Family Survey, who to send it to, sending it with an email campaign, reading the answers, and following up with families who share their name.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-13
Related: https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/forms-and-docs.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/marketing.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/smart-lists.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/visit-feedback-survey.md

About 60 days into a family's first year is the best time to ask how the start went. They know your school well enough to answer, and it's early enough to fix a problem before it turns into a family who doesn't re-enroll. Your account has a ready-made **New Family Survey** in **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, then the **Prebuilt Surveys** folder.

## When should we send the New Family Survey?

Send it about 60 days after a new family's first day of school. For families who started in August, that's late October. For a family who enrolled mid-year, it's about 60 days after their child's first day.

Send it once per family, only to families in their first year. Answers are anonymous unless a parent chooses to add their name, which makes families more honest.

To send it on its own every year, see [Have the survey send automatically](#have-the-survey-send-automatically).

## Mark your new families with a tag

A tag lets you send the survey to only your first-year families. Do this once each fall.

1. Ask the Banner 61 team to create a tag for this year's new families, such as **new family 2026-27**. New tags can start automations, so let Banner 61 add it.
2. Go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Smart Lists**, and open **Current Families**.
3. Check the box next to each family who started this school year.
4. Click **Add tags**, type an **Action name** such as "Tag new families 2026-27", pick the tag and click **Add tags**.

See [Tag every family on a list](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/bulk-actions/#tag-every-family-on-a-list) for more on tagging a group.

How to check it worked: go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, then **Bulk Actions**. Your job shows with the status Complete, and the tag shows on each family's record.

## Send the survey to your new families

Send the New Family Survey with an email campaign in **Marketing**, then **Emails**, so it looks polished and you can see who opened it.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, open the **Prebuilt Surveys** folder, and copy the share link for **New Family Survey**.
2. Go to **Marketing**, then **Emails**, then the **Campaigns** tab, and create a new campaign. The **Quick Announcement** template in the Campaigns folder is a good start.
3. Write a short, warm note: you're glad they're part of the school, you'd love to hear how the first two months went, and it takes a few minutes. Add a button that links to the survey.
4. Click **Send or Schedule**. Under recipients, choose the tag you added for this year's new families.
5. Schedule it for a weekday morning, and plan one reminder a week later to families who haven't opened it.

See [Email campaigns](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/marketing/#email-campaigns-one-time-emails-to-a-group) for every campaign setting.

How to check it worked: open the campaign's three-dot menu and choose **Statistics**. Delivered should match the number of new families you tagged.

## Have the survey send automatically

The Banner 61 team can set up an automation that sends the New Family Survey on its own: on a date you choose in the fall for families who enrolled before school started, and about 60 days after enrolling for families who join mid-year. Ask the Banner 61 team to turn it on and tell them the date you want.

Once it's on, you don't need to tag families or send a campaign each year. Check with the Banner 61 team before changing the automation, because it depends on your pipeline stages.

## Read what new families said

All answers are in **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, then the **Submissions** tab.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**.
2. Click the **Submissions** tab.
3. Choose **New Family Survey** in the survey filter and set the date range.
4. Click a response to read it.

Most responses are anonymous. A response shows a family's name only when the parent chose to share it at the end of the survey.

Read for patterns rather than single comments. If several families say communication is unclear, or the admissions process was confusing at one step, that's worth a change before next year's families arrive.

How to check it worked: the number of responses should grow in the days after each send. If nothing comes in after a week, check that the campaign link opens the survey.

## Follow up with a family who shared their name

The last question invites parents to share their name and email if they'd like someone to follow up. Treat every one of those as a request for a conversation.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Surveys**, then the **Submissions** tab, and filter to **New Family Survey**.
2. Open each response that has a name.
3. Call or email the family within a few days. Thank them, and respond to what they shared.
4. If someone else should handle it, such as a teacher or the head of school, add a task for that person on the family's contact. See [Tasks](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/tasks/).

Keep anonymous answers anonymous. Don't try to work out who wrote a response without a name.

How to check it worked: the family's contact shows your note, email or task.
