Schedule a post to Facebook and Instagram

Banner Connect help · Marketing > Social Planner · Reviewed 2026-11-16

You write a post once in Marketing, then Social Planner, pick your school's Facebook page and Instagram account, and post it now or schedule it for later. Connect your accounts first; see Connect your school's Facebook and Instagram.

Write and schedule a post

Start a new post from Marketing, then Social Planner, and choose Schedule post when it's ready.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Social Planner, and click New Post, then Create New Post.
  2. Under Post to, check your school's Facebook page, your Instagram account, or both.
  3. Type your post in the box. The preview on the right shows how it will look.
  4. To add a photo, click the image icon under the text box, pick a photo from Media Storage or click Upload to add one, and click Insert Files. Instagram posts need a photo or video.
  5. Click the arrow next to Post and choose Schedule post, then pick the date and time.

Don't click the main Post button unless you mean to publish right now; it goes out to your page right away.

How to check it worked: the post shows in Social Planner on the Planner tab with a scheduled status and the date you picked.

Save a post as a draft

To finish a post later, or to let someone else look it over, save it as a draft from Marketing, then Social Planner.

  1. Write the post as usual in Marketing, then Social Planner, then New Post.
  2. Click Save for later.

Drafts don't go anywhere until someone opens them and schedules or posts them.

How to check it worked: the post shows on the Planner tab with the status Draft.

Have someone approve a post before it goes out

If your head of school or communications person likes to see posts first, send it for approval from Marketing, then Social Planner.

  1. Write the post in Marketing, then Social Planner, then New Post.
  2. Click the arrow next to Post and choose Send Post for Approval.

How to check it worked: the post waits on the Planner tab until it's approved. If you're not sure who receives approval requests in your account, ask the Banner 61 team.

Change or delete a post that's already scheduled

Every draft and scheduled post is listed in Marketing, then Social Planner, on the Planner tab.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Social Planner, then the Planner tab. Use the calendar icon at the top to see the month, or the list icon for a list.
  2. Click the three dots on the post's row.
  3. Choose Edit to change it, Clone to start a similar post, or Delete to remove it.
  4. After editing, schedule it again or click Save for later.

If the post was already published, check your Facebook page or Instagram afterward to make sure it's gone, and delete it there if it isn't.

How to check it worked: the post shows the new wording and date on the Planner tab.

Post the same thing every week

For a weekly reminder, like a Friday chapel note or a Monday lunch menu, use a recurring post from Marketing, then Social Planner.

  1. Write the post in Marketing, then Social Planner, then New Post.
  2. Click the arrow next to Post and choose Schedule Recurring Posts.
  3. Set how often it repeats, then save it.

How to check it worked: the upcoming posts show on the Planner tab calendar.

What makes a good school post?

Lead with a real photo of students learning when you can. Keep the words short: one thing that happened, why it matters, and one next step for families.

  • Before an event, say the date, time and what families will do there, and add the sign-up link. For an open house, use your Event Registration link.
  • On Instagram, links in captions can't be clicked. Write "Link in bio" and put the link in your profile.
  • Use a parent's own words for parent stories, with their permission.
  • Check photo permissions before posting a student's face or name.

To turn an event post into a flyer too, see Make a QR code for your open house flyer.
