Collect a parent video story in five minutes

Banner Connect help · Marketing > Video Testimonials · Reviewed 2026-11-16

A parent video story is a short video, about 30 seconds, of a parent saying why they chose your school. You send the parent a link, they record on their phone, and the video lands in Marketing, then Video Testimonials, ready to put on your website.

What is a parent video story?

A parent video story is a short phone video where a current parent tells other families why they chose your school. Families who are still deciding trust another parent more than anything the school says about itself, and a plain 30-second video does that better than a polished promo.

Your account comes with one link already set up for this, called Parent Video Stories, in Marketing, then Video Testimonials. The parent doesn't need an app or an account. They open the link, answer a question or two on camera, add their name and send it. Videos can be up to two and a half minutes, but shorter is better. To send the link, see Send a parent the video link.

Send a parent the video link

Copy the Parent Video Stories link and send it to the parent in a personal email or text.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Video Testimonials, then the Video Collectors tab.
  2. On the Parent Video Stories card, click the three dots and choose Copy Link.
  3. Open the parent's contact in CRM, then Contacts, or go to CRM, then Conversations.
  4. Write a short, personal note and paste the link. For wording, see What should we say when we ask a parent?
  5. Send it.

Ask parents one at a time, in person first if you can ("Would you be willing to record a quick video for us? I'll send you the link."). A personal ask works far better than a group email.

How to check it worked: open the link yourself in a private browser window. You should see your school's name and a Let's Go! button. Don't submit a test video, because it would show up with the real ones.

What should we say when we ask a parent?

Keep the ask short and warm, say it takes about 30 seconds, and include a one-line permission statement. Here is wording you can copy and change:

"Hi [first name], we have a small favor to ask. Families thinking about our school love hearing from parents like you. Would you record a short video, about 30 seconds, on why your family chose us? You can do it right from your phone with this link, no app needed: [link]. Talk the way you would to a friend at pickup. It doesn't need to be polished. Thank you for being part of our school family."

Permission line to add at the end: "By sending your video, you give [school name] permission to share it on our website, social media and enrollment materials, and we'll take it down any time you ask."

To send it, see Send a parent the video link.

What should we ask parents to talk about?

The best questions are ones a parent can answer from the heart without preparing. These three work well for Christian schools:

  • Why did your family choose our school?
  • Tell us about a teacher who made a difference for your child.
  • What would you tell a family who is thinking about our school?

The Parent Video Stories link can hold up to three questions, and each question is its own short recording. One question is enough for a 30-second video. Pick the one that fits the parent best and mention it in your note.

To change the questions on the link, see Change the questions or the thank-you message.

Do we need permission to share a parent's video?

Yes. Only share a video when the parent has agreed to it being used publicly. The Parent Video Stories link asks every parent to tick a box agreeing that their video can be used for marketing, and your ask should include a plain permission line too (see What should we say when we ask a parent?).

A few good habits:

  • Check the consent before you use a video. In Marketing, then Video Testimonials, then Responses, use the Marketing consent filter to see which videos the parent agreed to share.
  • Students are minors. If a child appears in the video or is named, make sure the parent is comfortable with that before you share it.
  • If a parent asks you to take a video down, remove it from your website and social posts right away.

To show or hide a video on your website, see Choose which videos show on your website.

See the videos parents have sent

Every video a parent sends shows up in Marketing, then Video Testimonials, then the Responses tab.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Video Testimonials.
  2. Click the Responses tab.
  3. Use the Status and Marketing consent filters, or search by the parent's name.
  4. Open a response to watch the video.

The Overview tab also shows how many videos each link has collected and the most recent ones.

How to check it worked: after a parent tells you they've sent their video, their name should appear on the Responses tab.

Change the questions or the thank-you message

You edit the Parent Video Stories link in Marketing, then Video Testimonials.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Video Testimonials, then the Video Collectors tab.
  2. On the Parent Video Stories card, click the three dots and choose View & Edit Details.
  3. Choose the page to change: Welcome Page (the title and short welcome), Questions Page (up to three questions), Attribution Page (where the parent adds their name) or Thank You Page.
  4. Click Save.

The link stays the same after you edit it, so links you've already sent keep working. To change the logo or colors, ask the Banner 61 team.

How to check it worked: open the link in a private browser window and read each page as a parent would.

Why does the screen say "Send Review Request"?

Banner Connect doesn't use review requests, so you can ignore the Send Review Request button at the top of Marketing, then Video Testimonials. Its email and text options are turned off in your account, and it can't be hidden from the screen. To ask a parent for a video, copy the link and send a personal note instead. See Send a parent the video link.
