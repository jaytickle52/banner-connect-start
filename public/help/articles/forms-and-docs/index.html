Getting started

Forms, surveys and documents

Banner Connect help

The Forms & Docs tab has Forms, Surveys, and Documents & Contracts.

Your prebuilt forms

  • General Inquiry Form (contact form): a simple contact form.
  • Request Info Packet: the main inquiry form. Families who submit it get the Request Info Packet / Lead Nurture emails.
  • Request Tuition Info and Request Financial Aid Info: each sends its own information email.
  • Request a Visit
  • Event Registration Form: used by the Event Registration calendar.
  • Sibling Intent Form: for current families adding a sibling.

Every submission creates or updates the family's contact record and starts the matching automation. You can see all submissions in each form's submissions list.

Put your inquiry form on your website

  1. Go to Forms & Docs, then Forms, and open Request Info Packet (or General Inquiry Form for a simpler contact form).
  2. Click Integrate in the top right, choose the embed option, and click Copy.
  3. Paste the code on your website: - WordPress: add a Custom HTML block (Elementor: an HTML widget; Divi: a Code module). - Squarespace: a Code block. - Wix: Embed, then Embed HTML. Webflow and others: an Embed or custom code element.
  4. Publish the page and check it on the live site (some editors don't show embeds in preview).

If someone else manages your site, send them the code and tell them which page it goes on. If you'd rather not do it, share access to your website with Banner 61 and we'll add it.

Test it: fill out the form with your own name and email. Within a minute you should see yourself in CRM, then Opportunities, under New Inquiry, and the first email in your inbox. Delete the test contact afterward so it doesn't count in your reports.

No access to your website code? Use a form landing page instead: a ready-made page with the form on it that you link to from a button. Your account has landing pages for Request Info Packet, General Inquiry Form, Request Tuition Info and Request Financial Aid Info under Marketing, then Funnels. Open the page, copy its URL, and use it as the link on your site.

Edit a form

Open the form in Forms & Docs, then Forms. - Add a field: drag one in from the elements panel. Use Quick Add for a brand-new field, or Add Object Fields to reuse a field that already exists in your account. - Change a field: click it to change the label, placeholder text or whether it's required (or click the gear icon). - Reorder: drag fields into place. - Style: click the sliders icon at the top right to open Styles, Themes and Advanced (custom CSS). You can use one or two columns, set field widths, and adjust colors and fonts for your brand. - Show or hide questions based on answers: click the branching icon in the top toolbar (Conditional Logic). You can show or hide fields, show a custom message, send families to a page after they submit, or disqualify a submission. - Email alerts and auto-reply: click the bell icon to send your team an email on each submission, or an automatic reply to the family. - Click Save Form when you're done.

Good to know: - Reuse existing fields. If several forms ask "How did you hear about us?", use the same field on each so answers land in one place. If you see "A custom field already uses this name", remove the new field and add the existing one from Add Object Fields instead. - Your prebuilt forms already send automatic emails through workflows. Turning on the form's own auto-responder too may send families two replies. - Changing a form that's live on your website changes it for families right away.

Surveys

Go to Forms & Docs, then Surveys. The Visit Feedback survey sends automatically after a visit is marked as attended and a good fit. Review its questions and wording before your visits start. You can edit any survey yourself, or ask Banner 61 to.

Documents & Contracts

Go to Forms & Docs, then Documents & Contracts. Use it for enrollment agreements, permission slips, consent forms, waivers and handbook acknowledgments that families sign online.

Send a document to one family: create or open a template (your account includes an Enrollment Agreement template), send it to the family's contact, and they sign by email. Signed copies show under Completed.

Share a public document with anyone: 1. Go to Templates, click New, then Create Public Document. 2. Add fields: text, dates, checkboxes, dropdowns, signatures and initials. 3. Click Publish, then Generate Links, and copy the link. 4. Share it by email, text, on your website or as a QR code.

Signers don't need an account. They enter their name and email, fill in the fields, sign with a mouse or finger, and get a signed copy by email. One link works for any number of people until you unpublish it.

  • See who's signed: open Documents & Contracts and check the responses or Completed list. Use the three-dot menu to download a signed PDF.
  • Stop accepting signatures: unpublish the document. To edit it, unpublish, make changes and publish again.
  • Reuse it next year: duplicate the template and rename it (for example, "2026-27 Field Trip Waiver – Grade 5").
  • Get notified when someone signs: this needs a small workflow (Documents & Contracts trigger, status Signed, then an internal notification). Ask Banner 61 if you'd like one set up.
