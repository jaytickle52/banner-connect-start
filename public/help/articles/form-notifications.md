# Get an email when a family fills out a form

Menu: Forms & Docs > Forms > Notifications
Use when: Choosing who on your staff gets an email each time a family fills out a website form, and what to check when a family says they filled out a form but nobody at school heard about it.
Page for people: https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/form-notifications/
Last reviewed: 2026-11-17
Related: https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/form-submissions.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/forms-and-docs.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/website-forms.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/contacts.md

Each form in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, can email your staff the moment a family fills it out. Every form has its own setting on its **Notifications** tab, so check each form your school uses.

## Choose who gets an email when a family fills out a form

Open the form in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, click the **Notifications** tab and put your staff's email address in **Email (To)**.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**. The prebuilt forms are in the **Prebuilt Forms** folder.
2. Click the form, for example **General Inquiry Form (contact form)**.
3. Click the **Notifications** tab at the top of the form builder.
4. Make sure **Email notification** is switched on.
5. In **Email (To)**, enter the address that should hear about every submission, such as your admissions director or admissions@yourschool.org. You can add more than one address. The first one gets the email and the rest are copied.
6. Optional: fill in **Reply-to-email** and **Sender name**, and check **Send form submission as a PDF** if your team likes a copy of each one.
7. Click **Save** at the bottom of the Notifications tab.
8. Do the same for each form on your website: General Inquiry Form, Request Info Packet, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info, Request a Visit and the Event Registration Form.

How to check it worked: fill out the form on your live website with your own email address. The notification should reach the **Email (To)** address within a few minutes, and your entry shows on the **Submissions** tab in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**.

If **Email notification** is on but **Email (To)** is blank, nobody gets an email. Check this on every form, because a blank address is the most common reason a school doesn't hear about new families.

## A family says they filled out a form but we never heard about it

Look for the submission first: it's almost always in Banner Connect, and only the email to your staff went missing.

1. Go to **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, and click the **Submissions** tab.
2. Set the form picker at the top left to **All forms**.
3. Widen the dates at the top right to cover a few weeks.
4. Type the family's email or last name in **Search**.
5. If the submission isn't there, go to **CRM**, then **Contacts**, and search by the family's email and last name. A family who used two devices may have two records. To combine them, see [Duplicates](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/contacts/#duplicates).
6. Open the form's **Notifications** tab in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, and check that **Email (To)** has the right address. To fix it, see [Choose who gets an email when a family fills out a form](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/form-notifications/#choose-who-gets-an-email-when-a-family-fills-out-a-form).
7. Search your inbox, Spam and Promotions for the form's name. If the emails landed there, mark them Not spam so the next ones reach your inbox.
8. If you recently changed or rebuilt a form, your website may still show an older one. Fill out the form on your live website and confirm it appears on the **Submissions** tab.

How to check it worked: your test entry shows on the **Submissions** tab and the notification reaches your inbox. Then reach out to the family the same day.

Still can't find it? Send the Banner 61 team the family's name, email address and roughly when they filled it out.

## Should form emails go to a person or a shared inbox?

Either works, and many schools use both. A shared inbox like admissions@yourschool.org keeps working when staff change. A person's own inbox usually gets read faster. Since **Email (To)** takes more than one address, you can send each submission to both. After an event or a mailing, it also helps to look at the **Submissions** tab in **Forms & Docs**, then **Forms**, once a day for the first week. To set the addresses, see [Choose who gets an email when a family fills out a form](https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/form-notifications/#choose-who-gets-an-email-when-a-family-fills-out-a-form).
