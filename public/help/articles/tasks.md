# Tasks and notifications

Menu: CRM > Tasks; Dashboard; Settings > My Profile > Notifications
Use when: Creating and assigning follow-up tasks, due dates, getting notified when a task is assigned, and reading the Dashboard.
Page for people: https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/tasks/
Last reviewed: 2026-11-03
Related: https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/advanced-and-settings.md, https://demo.banner61.com/help/articles/reporting-and-payments.md

Tasks are internal to-dos with a due date and an owner: call a family back, send a tuition sheet, review an application. Families never see them.

## Where tasks live

- **CRM**, then **Tasks**: every task in the account. Filter to your own tasks, or by Due Today, Overdue or Upcoming.
- A contact record: the Tasks icon in the right panel.
- An opportunity: the Tasks section in its left sidebar.

A task created in one place shows up in the others.

## Create a task

1. Open the task list, a contact or an opportunity and click **+ Add Task**.
2. Enter a title that says what needs to happen ("Call the Smith family about the 2nd grade tour").
3. Add a description if it helps, a due date and time, and who it's **Assigned to**.
4. Turn on **Recurring** for work that repeats, like a weekly check of new inquiries.
5. Click **Save**.

Check a task off to complete it. Completed tasks stay on the family's record.

If a staff member isn't in the Assign to list, they need their own Banner Connect user first. Ask the Banner 61 team to add them.

## Turn on task notifications

Assigning a task doesn't alert anyone by itself. Each person chooses their own notifications:

1. Click **Settings**, then **My Profile**, then **Notifications**.
2. Open **Task Notifications**.
3. For each notification, check In-App, Email and/or SMS:
   - Notify when a task gets assigned to me
   - Reminder before an upcoming task (choose minutes, hours or days)
   - Daily summary of due tasks (choose the time)
   - Daily summary of overdue tasks (choose the time)
4. Click **Save** at the bottom right.

A good starting setup: assigned to me by In-App and Email; a reminder 1 hour before, In-App; both daily summaries at 8:00 AM by Email, with "Don't send notifications when there are no tasks" turned on.

Not getting notifications? Make sure you clicked Save, wait up to an hour for new reminders to start, check that the task is assigned to you with a due date, check the email and phone on your profile, and look in spam.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard shows enrollment at a glance: how many families reach each stage, the tuition still in the pipeline, and your tasks, with overdue ones in red. A good daily habit is to open it each morning and work your task list from the top.
