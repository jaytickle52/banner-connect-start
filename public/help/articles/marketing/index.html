Getting started

Marketing

Banner Connect help · Marketing · Reviewed 2026-11-13

The Marketing tab has Social Planner, Emails, Trigger Links, Video Testimonials, Funnels and QR Codes.

Email templates

Go to Marketing, then Emails, then the Templates tab. Templates are the saved emails your automations and campaigns send. Your account comes with folders of ready-made templates (see Before your forms go live: review and approve for what each folder does).

  • Edit a template: open it, change the wording, and save. Check tone, your admissions steps, visit instructions and your signature.
  • Make a new template: click New, choose a layout or start blank, drag in text, images, buttons and your logo, name it and save.
  • Copy a template: use the three-dot menu next to it and choose the clone option, then rename it.
  • Test before you use it: send yourself a test email to check the layout, links and names.

Personalize with merge fields (see Merge fields for the list). If a family has no first name on file, "Hi {{contact.first_name}}," arrives as "Hi ,". To avoid that, click the merge field in the email editor and add fallback text such as "there".

Name templates clearly (for example, "Tour Confirmation – Admissions") and keep them in folders.

Good to know: whether an edit to a template reaches emails an automation already sends depends on how that automation step was set up. After editing a template that an automation uses, send yourself a test through the automation or ask the Banner 61 team to confirm the change is live.

Email campaigns (one-time emails to a group)

Use a campaign for announcements, newsletters, event invitations and other one-time sends. It's the best way to email a group, because it looks polished and shows who opened and clicked.

Your account has a Campaigns template folder (Marketing, then Emails, then Templates) with four ready-to-edit starters: Open House Invitation, Monthly Family Newsletter, Re-enrollment Is Open and Quick Announcement. Each has your logo, signature and footer already in place. Replace every word in [BRACKETS] before you send.

  1. Go to Marketing, then Emails, then the Campaigns tab, and create a new campaign.
  2. Start from a template (one from the Campaigns folder is the quickest) or a blank layout, and build the email.
  3. Click Preview to check desktop and mobile, and send a test to yourself.
  4. Click Send or Schedule and fill in: - Sender name (such as "Admissions at Banner Christian School") and sender email. The sender email must use your school's verified sending address; ask Banner 61 if you're not sure which to use. - Subject line and preview text. - Recipients: individual contacts, tags, Smart Lists, a pre-built segment (like contacts who opened an email in the last 30 days), or a custom segment.
  5. Optional: turn on Track Clicks before sending (you can't add it later), UTM tracking, or tagging families based on what they click.
  6. Choose Send Now, Schedule (uses your account's time zone) or Batch Schedule for very large lists.

Attachments must total 10 MB or less. Families who unsubscribed or are set to Do Not Disturb are skipped automatically. To change a scheduled send, use the campaign's three-dot menu and choose Reschedule at least an hour before it goes out.

Custom segments: in the Recipients area choose Build Segments. Each rule has a field, a condition and a value (Tag is Prospective Family). Add Filter adds a rule that must also match (AND); Add OR Filter adds an alternative group (OR). Examples: Tag is Inquiry and Tag is not Tour Scheduled; Grade Level Applying For is Kindergarten. Save segments you'll reuse.

For a short, plain note to a list, you can also send straight from a contact list. See Send a quick email to a list.

Results: open the campaign's three-dot menu and choose Statistics, then Load More Statistics for clicks by link. For all email together, open the Statistics tab under Marketing, then Emails. Delivered means it reached the family's mail server, not that they opened it. Watch bounces, unsubscribes and spam complaints for list problems.

Trigger links

A trigger link takes the family to a page you choose, records the click on their contact record, and can start an automation.

Create one: go to Marketing, then Trigger Links, click + Add Link, give it a clear name ("Tuition PDF Link"), paste the destination address and save.

Use it: copy the link key with the copy icon and paste it as the link in an email button, snippet or text message. Pasting the plain web address instead won't be tracked.

Follow up automatically: a workflow with the Trigger Link Clicked trigger, set to that specific link, can tag the family, alert a staff member or send the next email. Ask Banner 61 if you'd like one set up.

Make one link per choice you want to measure ("View Tuition" and "Schedule a Tour" should be separate links). To see totals, open the link's three-dot menu and choose Analytics.

Funnels (landing pages)

Go to Marketing, then Funnels. Funnels are web pages hosted by Banner Connect, such as the form landing pages (Request Info Packet, General Inquiry Form, Request Tuition Info, Request Financial Aid Info) and pages for an open house, an ad or a scholarship. Open a page and copy its URL to link to it.

QR codes

Go to Marketing, then QR Codes.

  1. Click Create QR Code.
  2. Name it by what it does ("Schedule a Tour – Viewbook").
  3. Choose the type (website, form, survey, funnel page, payment link and more) and the destination. Forms and pages must already exist in your account.
  4. Optionally add your colors, a frame, a call to action like "Scan to Book a Tour", and your logo. Scan it in the builder to test.
  5. Download it: PDF for print, SVG for designers, PNG for digital use. Or copy a link to share it.

You can change where a code points later without reprinting: hover the code, click Edit and update the destination. (Codes made with the bulk CSV upload are plain codes; make a code one at a time if you'll want to change it later.)

Use one code per purpose (tour, request info, apply, RSVP, survey) so you can see which one families use. Hover a code and click Analytics to see scans over time, by device and by location. Organize codes in folders, and download before deleting; deleting a QR code is permanent.

Social Planner

Social Planner lets you schedule posts to Facebook, Instagram and other accounts from Banner Connect. It shows a connect screen until your school's social accounts are connected; someone at the school connects them, because it signs in to the school's own accounts.

Video Testimonials

Collect short videos from parents to use on your website. If you'd like help setting up a collection link, ask the Banner 61 team.
