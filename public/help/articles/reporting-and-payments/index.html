Getting started

Dashboard, reporting and payments

Banner Connect help · Dashboard; Reporting; Payments · Reviewed 2026-11-03

Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It shows how many families reach each stage and how many move on, the total tuition of families still deciding, and your tasks, with overdue ones in red. Open it each morning and work your task list from the top.

Each user can arrange their own dashboard without changing anyone else's: use the edit or customize option on the Dashboard to add, remove and drag widgets. An admissions director might keep the pipeline and upcoming visits at the top; a head of school might prefer enrollment progress and lead sources.

Reporting

The Reporting tab has: - Reports: lead sources, appointments and email results. - Custom Reports: reports built for your school. - SEO: how your website shows up in search. - Site Analytics: visits to your Banner Connect forms, pages and QR codes. - External Tracking: tracking codes for outside tools.

A good question to answer once a term: which lead source sent you the most families this year (your website, Facebook, Google, a referral)? That's where your next hour of marketing should go. Open Reporting, then Reports, and look at lead sources.

For email results, see Email campaigns. For QR code scans, hover the code in Marketing, then QR Codes, and click Analytics.

Payments

Use Payments for application fees, enrollment deposits and invoices. The tab includes Invoices & Estimates, Payment Links, Transactions, Subscriptions, Products and Payment Integrations.

Connect a payment account first. Ask the Banner 61 team before you send any invoice or payment link. An admin with access to the school's payment accounts connects the account in Payments, then Payment Integrations: - Stripe (recommended for most schools): click Connect with Stripe, sign in or create an account, and authorize it. - PayPal: click Connect PayPal and sign in to your school's PayPal Business account. - Authorize.net, NMI and Square are also supported.

You can connect more than one, but one is the default for new payments. Only payments made after connecting show in your reports. Run a test payment first if your gateway offers a test mode.

  • Payment link: a link for an application fee or deposit that you can send by email or text, or turn into a QR code.
  • Invoice: send a bill to a family and let them pay online.
  • Transactions: every payment in one place.

If you switch gateways later, existing subscriptions keep running through the original one, so don't disconnect the old gateway.
