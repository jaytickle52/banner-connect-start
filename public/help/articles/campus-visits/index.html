Getting started

Let families book a campus visit

Banner Connect help · Calendars and Events > Calendars > Meeting Calendars · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Families can book a campus visit themselves from a link on your website or in your emails, at times you choose. The visit lands on the right staff calendar, the family moves to Visit Scheduled in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline, and the reminders and follow-up go out on their own. Visits use the Schedule a Visit calendar in Calendars and Events, then Calendars, then Meeting Calendars.

Set your visit hours

Your visit hours are the days and times families can pick from on the Schedule a Visit calendar.

  1. Go to Calendars and Events, then Calendars, then Meeting Calendars.
  2. Click the pencil next to Schedule a Visit.
  3. Open Availability. Set the days and hours you give visits, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 9:00 to 2:00.
  4. Check the meeting length. Schedule a Visit is set to 60 minutes.
  5. To block a single day, like a school holiday or testing day, click Add Date Specific Hours, pick the date and mark it unavailable.
  6. Save.

How to check it worked: open your booking link in a private browser window and look at next week. The times you see should match the hours you set, in your school's time zone. If every time is off by a few hours, ask the Banner 61 team to check the calendar's time zone.

Choose who gives the visit

The staff member assigned to Schedule a Visit is the one whose calendar each visit lands on, and who gets the follow-up email afterward.

  1. Go to Calendars and Events, then Calendars, then Meeting Calendars.
  2. Click the pencil next to Schedule a Visit.
  3. Under team members, choose the person who gives visits. If two people share visits, ask the Banner 61 team to set the calendar up for more than one person.
  4. Save.

That person should connect their own calendar so visits don't land on top of meetings. See Connect your calendar.

How to check it worked: open your booking link and confirm open times appear. If no times appear, the staff member may have no free time in their own connected calendar during visit hours.

Put the visit link on your website and in emails

Your Schedule a Visit booking link is the one link to use everywhere a family might want to visit.

  1. Go to Calendars and Events, then Calendars, then Meeting Calendars.
  2. Open the share options next to Schedule a Visit.
  3. Copy the booking link to paste into an email, a text or a button on your website. Or copy the embed code to put the calendar right on a page of your website.

If someone else manages your website, send them the embed code. The Banner 61 team can also add it for you.

How to check it worked: click the link or open the website page in a private browser window and make sure you can see open times.

What happens after a family books a visit?

As soon as a family books on Schedule a Visit, Banner Connect confirms it, moves the family to Visit Scheduled in the Inquiry to Enrollment pipeline, and sends a confirmation email. A reminder goes out the day before and another an hour before. The family sees a short "You're booked" message on screen right away, so they know nothing else is needed from them.

After the visit, the staff member gets an email with three links: they showed up, they didn't show, or they showed but aren't a fit. Clicking one marks the visit and sends the family the right next email. A family marked as showing up also gains 15 points on their engagement score.

These steps run from the Request a Visit workflow. See After a visit: the staff follow-up email. Check with the Banner 61 team before changing that workflow.

Book a visit for a family who called

When a family calls or stops by, book the visit for them on Schedule a Visit so they get the same emails as a family who booked online. See Book a visit for a family (phone or walk-in).

Change the message families see after booking

The message families see after they book is set on the Schedule a Visit calendar.

  1. Go to Calendars and Events, then Calendars, then Meeting Calendars.
  2. Click the pencil next to Schedule a Visit.
  3. Find the confirmation or thank-you message setting and edit the text. Keep it short: what happens next, how long the visit is, and how to reschedule.
  4. Save.

How to check it worked: book a test visit with your own email, read the message, then cancel the test visit from Calendars and Events, then Calendars, then Appointment List.
