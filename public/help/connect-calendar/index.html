Getting started

Connect your calendar

Connecting your Google or Outlook calendar keeps online bookings from landing on top of things you already have scheduled, and puts new visits on the calendar you already check.

About 5 minutes
  1. 1

    Open Connections

    In the menu, go to Calendars, then Connections.

  2. 2

    Connect Google or Outlook

    Click Connect for the calendar you use and sign in with the email address you use for appointments.

  3. 3

    Choose which calendars count

    Pick the calendars Banner Connect should check for conflicts, so personal and school events block those times. Then pick the calendar new visits should be added to.

  4. 4

    Check your available times

    Go to Calendars, then Meeting Calendars, and open Schedule a Visit. Make sure the days and hours match when you actually give visits.

How to know it worked

Open your booking page and look at tomorrow. Any time you're already busy on your own calendar should be missing from the list.
