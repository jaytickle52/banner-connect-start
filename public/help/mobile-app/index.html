Getting started

Install the mobile app

The mobile app lets you answer families and see new inquiries from your phone.

About 5 minutes
  1. 1

    Download the app

    Search for LeadConnector in the App Store or Google Play and install it.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Use the same email and password you use for Banner Connect. If you have more than one account, choose your school.

  3. 3

    Turn on notifications

    Allow notifications when the app asks, so you hear about new messages and inquiries right away.

How to know it worked

Open Conversations in the app and reply to a message. The reply should show up on your computer too.
